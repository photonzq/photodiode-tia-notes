## Input type

bipolar ('classic' differential input, PDF p1; 'very high-speed complementary bipolar process', PDF p19, located by text)

## Compensation

decompensated voltage feedback ('two gain-stage design', medium gain); flat at G = +5, peaking 3.5 dB at G = +3

## Minimum stable gain

- **value**: 3
- **unit**: V/V (noise gain)
- **datasheet_statements**:
  - 'Operating at a noise gain less than +3 runs the risk of sustained oscillation (loop instability)' (PDF p14).
  - Table: small-signal bandwidth at G = +3 500 MHz typ, peaking at a gain of +3 3.5 dB typ (PDF p3).
  - 'unity-gain stability is not required for transimpedance applications' (PDF p11).
  - External compensation below the minimum gain: noninverting resistor across the inputs and inverting low-gain compensation with NG2 = 1 + CS/CF > minimum stable gain (example target NG2 = 7.5) (PDF p14).
- **src**: PDF pp. 3, 11, 14
