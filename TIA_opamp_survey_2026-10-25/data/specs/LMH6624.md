# LMH6624 — Texas Instruments ultra-low-noise wideband voltage-feedback op amp (single; LMH6626 = dual)

- **Source:** `datasheets/lmh6624.pdf`, SNOSA42G (November 2002, revised December 2014), PRODUCTION DATA. Package addendum dated 9-Jan-2026.
- **Citations:** pN is the PDF page index. It equals the printed page for p1–p28; p29–p43 are the addendum, packaging, mechanical and legal pages.
- **Value types:**
  - TYP = typical at 25 °C.
  - MIN/MAX = guaranteed at 25 °C ("over temp" = −40…125 °C).
  - CURVE = plot readout: vector sampling, or pixel tracing of an embedded raster.
  - SIM = simulation result.
  - TEXT = prose.
  - DERIVED = computed here.
- **Units:** values are for the single LMH6624 unless marked LMH6626.
- **Machine-readable record:** `LMH6624.json`.

Two of these rows matter more than the rest for a large-swing transimpedance amplifier:

- **Input common-mode range does not include $V^-$:** it needs about 1.5–2 V of headroom above $V^-$ and about 0.6–0.75 V below $V^+$.
- **Output swing is large:** typically ±4.9 V into 100 Ω on ±6 V.

What the open-loop curves imply:

- **Unity-gain crossover:** about 0.5 GHz, where the phase is about −250°. The part is not unity-gain stable.
- **At $|A_{OL}|$ = 20 dB** (noise gain 10, the minimum stable gain): about 100 MHz with phase −130°, i.e. phase margin ≈ 50° (DERIVED).
- **At 26 dB** (noise gain 20): about 52 MHz with phase ≈ −110° (±2.5 V).
- **Gain-bandwidth from these curves (DERIVED):**
  - ±6 V: 41.2 dB = 115 V/V at 10 MHz → 1.15 GHz; 20 dB at 100 MHz → 1.0 GHz.
  - ±2.5 V: 1.09 / 0.99 GHz.
  - Both are lower than the stated 1.5 GHz; see §7.

## 4. Noise (p9, p5, p7)

Figs 1 and 2 are traced from the embedded rasters (±3–4 %; the ±2.5 V and ±6 V traces overlap):

| f | $e_n$ (nV/√Hz) | $i_n$ (pA/√Hz) |
|---|---|---|
| 110 Hz | 4.4 | 11.3 |
| 1.1 kHz | 1.84 | 6.4 |
| 11 kHz | 1.15 | 2.2 |
| 110 kHz | 1.02 | 1.88 |
| 1.1 MHz | 1.02 | 1.90 |
| 9.3 MHz (highest plotted ≈ 10 MHz) | 1.02 | 1.81 |

- **Table values (TYP, f = 1 MHz):** $e_n$ = 0.92 nV/√Hz and $i_n$ = 2.3 pA/√Hz.
- **Mismatch with the curves:** the plotted flat levels (≈1.0 nV/√Hz, ≈1.85 pA/√Hz) match the LMH6626 table values instead (1.0, 1.8).
- **High-frequency $i_n$:** flat to 10 MHz, with no rise; nothing is given above 10 MHz.
- **$1/f$ corners (DERIVED):** ≈2.8 kHz for $e_n$, ≈7 kHz for $i_n$.
- **Noise model (p18–p19):**
  - $e_{ni}$ is dominated by $e_n$ below 26 Ω, by resistor thermal noise from 26 Ω to 3.1 kΩ, and by $i_n$ above that. The $e_n$ and $i_n$ terms are equal at 283 Ω.
  - Example: +20 V/V, 90 MHz, $R_{seq}$ = 25 Ω gives $e_{ni}$ = 1.3 nV/√Hz and 309 µVrms output noise.

## 5. Transimpedance-amplifier content (p23–p25)

- **Circuit (Fig 57, "LMH6624 Application Schematic"):**
  - +5 V single supply, $R_F$ = 1.2 kΩ, $C_F$ = 1.1 pF, $C_D$ = 10 pF.
  - IN+ at 3 V (2 kΩ / 3 kΩ ‖ 0.1 µF); $R_L$ = 500 Ω; $V_{OUT}$ = 3 V − 1200 × $I_D$.
- **Design equations:** Eq 8 $C_F = \sqrt{C_{IN}/(2\pi\,\mathrm{GBWP}\,R_F)}$ and Eq 9 $f_{-3dB} \approx \sqrt{\mathrm{GBWP}/(2\pi R_F C_{IN})}$, with $C_{IN} = C_D + C_{CM} + C_{DIFF}$.
- **Stated bandwidth:** "around 124 MHz in this case, assuming GBWP = 1.5 GHz, $C_{CM}$ = 0.9 pF, and $C_{DIFF}$ = 2 pF". This is calculated; there is no measured TIA curve. $C_F$ is "a starting point ... often less than 1 pF".
- **Check of the stated bandwidth (DERIVED):**
  - $C_{IN}$ = 12.9 pF.
  - $f_{-3dB} = \sqrt{1.5\times10^9/(2\pi \times 1200 \times 12.9\times10^{-12})}$ = 124 MHz.
  - $C_F$ = 1.07 pF.
  - Both are consistent with the text.
- **Noise gain (Fig 58):** $NG = (1+sR_F(C_{IN}+C_F))/(1+sR_FC_F)$, with high-frequency value $1 + C_{IN}/C_F$. The datasheet does not say that this must stay ≥ 10.
- **TIA current noise (Eq 10):** $i_{ni} = \sqrt{i_n^2 + (e_n/R_F)^2 + 4kT/R_F}$. Fig 61 (vector) gives:

| $R_F$ | Total $i_{ni}$ (pA/√Hz) |
|---|---|
| 1 kΩ | 4.74 |
| 1.5 kΩ | 4.02 |
| 2 kΩ | 3.65 |

  - The $i_n$ line in Fig 61 is at 2.26 pA/√Hz.
- **Text (p25):** "for $R_F$ < 3 kΩ, the noise performance is entirely dominated by $R_F$ thermal noise". The noise analysis "has ignored the possible noise gain increase, due to photo-diode capacitance, at higher frequencies."
- **Fig 59 is the wrong part:** it is numerically identical to LMH6629 Fig 62 ($i_n$ = 2.6 pA/√Hz, $e_n$ = 0.69 nV/√Hz). Fig 61 is the LMH6624 version.

## 6. Packages, pinout, layout, abs max

- **SOT-23-5 (DBV0005A):**
  - 2.90 × 1.60 mm nominal, 0.95 mm pitch, 1.45 mm max height, no exposed pad, $\theta_{JA}$ = 265 °C/W.
  - Pins: 1 OUT, 2 $V^-$, 3 +IN, 4 −IN, 5 $V^+$.
  - Orderables MF/MFX, marking A94A; MSL 1 (p3, p4, p29, p37).
- **SOIC-8 (D0008A):**
  - 4.90 × 3.91 mm nominal, 1.27 mm pitch, 1.75 mm max height, no exposed pad, $\theta_{JA}$ = 166 °C/W.
  - Pins: 2 −IN, 3 +IN, 4 $V^-$, 6 OUT, 7 $V^+$; pins 1, 5, 8 are not connected.
  - Orderables MA/MAX (p3, p4, p29, p34).
- **LMH6626 (dual):** SOIC-8 and VSSOP-8 (DGK, 3 × 3 mm, $\theta_{JA}$ = 235 °C/W).
- **Layout (p26–p27):**
  - Ceramic decoupling < 0.5 inch from the pins: 1000 pF–0.1 µF plus 4.7–10 µF tantalum.
  - Ground plane, removed near the inputs; short $R_F$/$R_G$ traces.
  - On the EVMs (LMH730216 for SOT-23-5, LMH730227 for SOIC-8), $R_F$ and $R_G$ are on the board bottom to cut summing-junction parasitics.
  - Use at least 25 Ω in series with +IN when it is driven from a 0 Ω source; bias-cancellation resistor $R_b$ ≥ 25 Ω (p17).
- **Absolute maximum (p4):**
  - Differential input **±1.2 V**; supply 13.2 V; inputs $V^+$ + 0.5 V to $V^-$ − 0.5 V; input current ±10 mA.
  - $T_J$ 150 °C; storage −65 to 150 °C.
  - ESD: HBM ±2000 V, MM ±200 V.
  - Clamp structure not described.

## 7. Uncertain or inconsistent items

1. **GBW:** 1.5 GHz in the text versus 1.0–1.15 GHz from the open-loop plots (Figs 7, 8: ≈41 dB at 10 MHz, ≈20 dB at 100 MHz, unity gain ≈ 0.5 GHz). Closed-loop high-gain data (Fig 6) give 1.4–1.7 GHz, which is closer to the text.
2. **Noise curves:** Figs 1–2 show ≈1.0 nV/√Hz and ≈1.85 pA/√Hz, which are the LMH6626 table values, not the LMH6624's 0.92 / 2.3.
3. **TIA noise plot:** Fig 59 is a copy of the LMH6629 plot.
4. **Offset drift:** ±0.1 µV/°C in the description versus ±0.2 (±6 V) and ±0.25 µV/°C (±2.5 V) in the tables.
5. **Input capacitance:** both values are simulation results.
6. **Input common-mode range:** only implied by the CMRR test conditions.
7. **Not read:** p2, p11, p13 and p15 (frequency response vs $V_{IN}$, sinking current, $I_{OS}$ vs supply, crosstalk, pulse response, PSRR vs f). Figs 31–36 (distortion vs f, gain and swing) were viewed but not digitized.
