# LT6200-10 (with LT6200-5 where different) — specification sheet (TIA survey)

**Datasheet:** `datasheets/lt6200_family_distributor_copy.pdf`, Linear Technology family data sheet LT6200/LT6200-5/LT6200-10/LT6201, document code **62001ff, Rev F (12/11)** (revision history p25; footer "LT 1211 REV F"). Production data sheet (no preliminary marking). Full record: `LT6200-10.json`.

**Citation format:** "p7" = PDF page 7 = printed page 7 (identical numbering, pages 1–26). Figure IDs are the data sheet's own (G67 etc.).

**Value types:** TYP (25 °C typical); MIN/MAX (guaranteed; 25 °C unless a range is named: 0–70 °C = C grade, −40–85 °C = I grade); CURVE (vector-path digitization of a typical curve, error < ±0.2 dB / ±0.5° unless marked "eye"); SCOPE (read from an oscilloscope photo).

**Scope of the data sheet for the -10:** the electrical tables do not separate family members except for GBW, slew rate, FPBW and settling; DC, noise, input capacitance, swing and supply-current entries apply to all members ("identical DC specifications", p20).

## 8. Protection and abs max
Back-to-back input diodes (±0.7 V), no internal series resistors; limit continuous diode current to 40 mA. A 100 Ω resistor in series with each input would raise noise from 0.95 to 2.03 nV/√Hz (p20). ESD diodes to both rails on all pins; transient current ≤ 30 mA beyond the rails is safe (Note 12). Output short circuit indefinite (heat sink may be needed). Thermal shutdown at 160 °C (to ~1.2 mA), restart near 150 °C (p22).

## 9. TIA-related content
- p1 "Single Supply, 1.5 nV/√Hz Photodiode Amplifier": unity-gain **LT6200** (not -10) with a BF862 JFET source follower (1 kΩ source resistor) inside the loop, +IN at 2.5 V, $R_F \parallel C_F$ to the JFET gate, $V_{OUT} \approx 2\,\text{V} + I_{PD}R_F$. No component values, photodiode capacitance or bandwidth given.
- "Transimpedance Amplifiers" is listed under Applications (p1). No TIA equations or compensation guidance elsewhere.

## 10. Data-sheet inconsistencies and caveats
- Phase margin at 25 °C: G62 gives 58.4° (±5 V) but G69 gives 65.2° (10 V); LT6200-5 shows the same ~5–7° gap (G45 vs G52). G67 gives 65.4° at the 20 dB (gain-10) crossing.
- G78 (LT6200-10) and G61 (LT6200-5) high-frequency noise spectra are the same trace, without stated conditions.
- G18 shows 1.80 nV/√Hz at 10 kHz (5 V) against 1.5 TYP in the table (table changed in Rev D).
- p9: the 20 mA $V_{OH}$ row is labelled $I_{SINK}$ (should be $I_{SOURCE}$); p8 "Setting Time"; p22 Table 2 titled "LT6200 8-Lead DD Package" although DD is LT6201-only.
- Not digitized (outside the scope of this record): G11 short-circuit vs supply, G12–G14 DC gain transfer, G46/G48/G54 LT6200-5 slew/PSRR/CMRR curves, LT6200 (unity-gain) curves p13–15.
