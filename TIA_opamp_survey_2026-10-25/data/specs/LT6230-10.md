## 8. Protection and abs max
Back-to-back input diodes (±0.7 V) without series resistors; limit to 40 mA (25 Ω per volt beyond 0.7 V); a 100 Ω series resistor per input would raise noise from 1.1 to 2.1 nV/√Hz (p17). Output can phase-invert at $A_V \ge 2$ under very heavy overdrive; keep inputs within 0.5 V beyond the rails (p18). ESD diodes to the rails tolerate transient currents up to 100 mA (p18). Output short circuit indefinite; $T_J$ 150 °C.

## 9. TIA-related content
- **p24 "Low Power Avalanche Photodiode Transimpedance Amplifier" (measured), unity-gain LT6230 (not -10):** $R_F$ = 1.5 kΩ, $C_F$ = 4.7 pF, APD (Advanced Photonix 012-70-62-541) cathode at ≈200 V, ±5 V supplies, $I_S$ = 3.3 mA, $R_2$ = 1.5 kΩ ∥ 0.1 µF on +IN for bias-current cancellation. Bandwidth 20 MHz (17 ns rise time), output noise 1.1 mV$_{pp}$ in 100 MHz bandwidth. Photodiode capacitance not stated. Output offset: "500 µV typical" (figure) versus "measured at 280 µV" (text).
- Text: the 1.5 kΩ resistor contributes 5 nV/√Hz or 3.4 pA/√Hz versus 1.1 nV/√Hz and 2.4 pA/√Hz from the amplifier.
