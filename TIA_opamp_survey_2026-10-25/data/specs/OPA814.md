**Open-loop gain/phase** (Fig. 6-23, p10, annotated $R_L$ = 1 GΩ; CURVE): 101 dB/−23° (1 kHz), 69.7/−89 (100 kHz), 49.7/−90 (1 MHz), 29.7/−91 (10 MHz), 20.1/−92 (30 MHz), 9.7/−98 (100 MHz), 3.7/−106 (200 MHz), 0.1/−114 (300 MHz), −4.6/−130 (500 MHz), −8.0/−145 (700 MHz). 0 dB at 303 MHz → unity-gain PM ≈ 66° (DERIVED).

**Noise curves** (p10): $e_n$ (Fig. 6-20, 10 Hz–1 MHz): 75 nV/√Hz @10 Hz, 24.7 @100 Hz, 9.3 @1 kHz, 5.9 @10 kHz, 5.45 @100 kHz, 5.40 @1 MHz. $i_n$ (Fig. 6-21, 10 kHz–100 MHz, no simulation label): 1.3 fA/√Hz @20 kHz, 5.0 @100 kHz, 51 @1 MHz, 0.53 pA/√Hz @10 MHz, 1.7 @30 MHz, 2.65 @50 MHz, ~3.9 pA/√Hz near 100 MHz — ∝ $f$ to ~30 MHz. DERIVED: $i_n/(e_n 2\pi f)$ ≈ 1.5 pF at 1 and 10 MHz.

**Swing vs current** (Fig. 6-26, 25 °C): +3.79/−3.81 V @0.5 mA, +3.36/−3.38 V @50 mA, +3.05/−3.03 V @80 mA.

Uncertain/contradictory: EC $i_n$ = 11 fA/√Hz for $f > 100$ kHz conflicts with the rising curve (5 fA/√Hz at 100 kHz, 0.5 pA/√Hz at 10 MHz); p1 text says SOIC-only although DBV is listed; $A_{OL}$ plot uses $R_L$ = 1 GΩ.
