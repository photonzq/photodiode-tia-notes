**Source:** `datasheets/ad8045_distributor_copy.pdf`, Analog Devices Data Sheet AD8045, Rev. C (12/2018), document code D04814-0-12/18(C). Production data sheet.

**Citation convention.** "pN" is the PDF page index; in this file it equals the printed page number ("Rev. C | Page N of 24"). Value types: **TYP**; **MIN/MAX** are guaranteed at $T_A = 25\,^\circ$C (the datasheet gives no over-temperature limits); **CURVE** values were read from typical-characteristic plots by sampling the plot's vector path after calibrating it against the gridlines, then checked against 160/300/600 dpi renders; **DERIVED** values are computed here and are not printed in the datasheet. Unless stated otherwise, conditions are those of Table 1: $V_S = \pm 5$ V, $G = +1$, $R_S = 100\ \Omega$, $R_L = 1\ \mathrm{k\Omega}$ to ground, 25 °C.

**Capacitive / transimpedance feedback.** The datasheet contains no TIA example and no guidance on capacitive feedback or noise-gain shaping. The only related statement (p22, Grounding): "Stray capacitance on the inverting input and the feedback resistor form a pole, which degrades the phase margin, leading to instability." It also advises removing ground and power planes under the input and output pins and, at unity gain, using a short, wide trace between FEEDBACK and −IN.

**Closed-loop bandwidth equation (p17):** $f_{-3\,\mathrm{dB}} = f_\mathrm{CROSSOVER}\, R_G/(R_G+R_F)$, stated to be valid for noise gains above +2.

## 6. Packages and pinout

| Package | Body | Pitch | Exposed pad | θJA / θJC (°C/W) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 8-lead SOIC_N_EP (RD-8-1, JEDEC MS-012-AA) | 4.90 × 3.90 mm, 6.00 mm across leads, 1.35–1.75 mm high | 1.27 mm | 2.29 × 2.29 mm | 80 / 30 |
| 8-lead LFCSP (CP-8-13, MO-229-WEED-4) | 3.00 × 3.00 × 0.75 mm | 0.50 mm | 1.74 × 1.45 mm (nominal) | 93 / 35 |

The exposed paddle is internally connected to −VS; it must be connected to −VS or left floating. Soldering it to ground shorts −VS to ground and "can cause irreparable damage" (p23). θJA is for a JEDEC 4-layer board; with an isolated paddle it approaches 125 °C/W. Sources: p5 Table 4, p6, p23, p24.

Pinout (p6 Table 5): SOIC 1 FEEDBACK, 2 −IN, 3 +IN, 4 −VS, 5 NIC, 6 OUTPUT, 7 +VS, 8 NIC. LFCSP 1 NIC, 2 FEEDBACK, 3 −IN, 4 +IN, 5 −VS, 6 NIC, 7 OUTPUT, 8 +VS. FEEDBACK is a dedicated pin tied to the output, placed next to −IN for a short feedback path; using it is optional. The LFCSP pinout separates +IN from −VS for lower HD2 (p19). Ordering: AD8045ARDZ(-REEL7), AD8045ACPZ-R2/-REEL7 (marking H8B), −40 °C to +125 °C.

## 7. Absolute maximum ratings (p5 Table 3)

Supply 12.6 V; common-mode input −VS − 0.7 V to +VS + 0.7 V; differential input ±VS; exposed paddle at −VS; junction 150 °C; operating −40 to +125 °C; storage −65 to +125 °C. No input protection diodes are described.

## 8. Inconsistencies and caveats in the datasheet

1. Supply current: 15 mA on the front page, 16 mA typ in Table 1.
2. Slew rate: 1350 V/µs in Table 1, 1400 V/µs in the Theory of Operation (p17).
3. Fig. 66 (p18) is captioned "Amplifier DC Errors" but shows the noise sources.
4. The Fig. 36 legend "RC = 1kΩ OR RC = 150kΩ" probably means $R_L$ = 1 kΩ or 150 Ω.
5. The Fig. 58 curve labels look swapped relative to Tables 1–2: Table 1 implies 1.4 V headroom at 100 Ω on ±5 V, Table 2 implies 1.2 V on +5 V, while the figure shows 1.15 V for the curve labelled ±5 V and 1.35 V for the one labelled +5 V. The figure does not state its load reference.
6. Input bias current is positive in the tables and negative in Fig. 55.
7. The Fig. 48 input impedance implies ≈ 4.3 pF, compared with the 1.3 pF common-mode input capacitance in the tables.
8. The current-noise plot falls slightly above 20 MHz and stops at 100 MHz. Its measurement method is not given.
