COMP HI reduces the compensation, which raises the slew rate and large-signal bandwidth at the higher gains (p20).

**Operation below the minimum gain** (p20–p22, Fig 50): put a series $R_c$–$C_c$ between the inputs to raise the high-frequency noise gain, subject to

- Constraint 1: $(1+R_f/R_g)\,(1+(R_p+R_{EQ})/R_c) \ge 10$ V/V (or ≥ 4 V/V for WSON-8 with COMP LO);
- Constraint 2: $1/(2\pi C_c R_c) \le 90$ MHz.

Here $R_p = R_s \| R_T$ and $R_{EQ} = R_f \| R_g$.

- **Worked example (SOT-23-5, +3.7 V/V):** $R_f$ = 249 Ω, $R_g$ = 91 Ω, $R_s = R_T$ = 50 Ω, giving $R_c \approx$ 56 Ω and $C_c \approx$ 33 pF. The text quotes a measured −3 dB of "about 1 GHz".
- **Reading of Fig 51:** 2.9 dB peak near 0.53 GHz, back through 0 dB at 1.02 GHz, −3 dB at 1.22 GHz.
- **Extra network in that measurement:** $C_f'$ = 1.5 pF across $R_f$ through a T network ($R_A$ = 33 Ω, $R_B$ = 91 Ω). Per the datasheet, WSON-8 does not need it.
- **Side effects:** the network lowers the input impedance (−3 dB at 490 MHz). The noise-gain zero is $1/(2\pi(R_c+R_p+R_{EQ})C_c)$ (Eq 1); noise gain is up 3 dB at 32.8 MHz.

## 9. Transimpedance-amplifier content

- **Circuit (p1 and Fig 60, p28, "200 MHz Transimpedance Amplifier Configuration"):**
  - Single +5 V supply, $R_F$ = 1.2 kΩ, $C_F$ = 0.6 pF (two 1.2 pF in series), $C_D$ = 10 pF.
  - IN+ biased at 3 V by 2 kΩ / 3 kΩ ‖ 0.1 µF; $R_L$ = 500 Ω; $V_{OUT}$ = 3 V − 1200 × $I_D$.
- **Design equations (p29):**
  - $C_F = \sqrt{C_{IN}/(2\pi\,\mathrm{GBWP}\,R_F)}$ (Eq 8).
  - $f_{-3dB} \approx \sqrt{\mathrm{GBWP}/(2\pi R_F C_{IN})}$ (Eq 9).
  - Total capacitance: $C_{IN} = C_D + C_{CM} + C_{DIFF}$.
- **Stated bandwidth:** "around 200 MHz in this case (assuming GBWP = 4GHz with COMP pin = HI for WSON-8 package)". This is calculated, not measured; no TIA frequency response is shown.
- **Tuning remark:** $C_F$ is "a starting point ... often less than 1 pF and thus is easily affected by board parasitics. For maximum speed, the LMH6629 COMP pin should be HI (or use the SOT-23 package)."
- **Check of the stated bandwidth (DERIVED):**
  - $C_{IN}$ = 10 + 1.7 + 4 = 15.7 pF.
  - $f_{-3dB} = \sqrt{4\times10^{9}/(2\pi \times 1200 \times 15.7\times10^{-12})}$ = 184 MHz.
  - $C_F = \sqrt{15.7\times10^{-12}/(2\pi\times4\times10^{9}\times1200)}$ = 0.72 pF.
  - Both agree with the stated ≈200 MHz and 0.6 pF.
- **Noise-gain sketch (Fig 61):** $NG = (1+sR_F(C_{IN}+C_F))/(1+sR_FC_F)$, with $f_Z \approx 1/(2\pi R_F C_{IN})$, $f_P = 1/(2\pi R_F C_F)$ and high-frequency $NG = 1 + C_{IN}/C_F$.
- **TIA input current noise (Eq 10, Fig 62, p29):** $i_{ni} = \sqrt{i_n^2 + (e_n/R_F)^2 + 4kT/R_F}$.

| $R_F$ | Total $i_{ni}$ (pA/√Hz) | Thermal | $e_n/R_F$ | $i_n$ |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 kΩ | 4.95 | — | — | — |
| 1.5 kΩ | 4.30 | 3.37 | 0.49 | 2.6 |
| 2 kΩ | 3.95 | — | — | — |
| 2.5 kΩ | 3.73 | — | — | — |

- **Text (p30):** below $R_F$ = 2.5 kΩ the noise is dominated by $R_F$ thermal noise, and $e_n$ never matters. However, "This noise analysis has ignored the possible noise gain increase, due to photo-diode capacitance, at higher frequencies."
- **No explicit minimum-gain rule for the TIA:** in practice $1 + C_{IN}/C_F$ at crossover must exceed 10 (COMP HI) or 4 (COMP LO). The datasheet only implies this through Fig 61.

## 10. Packages, pinout, layout

- **WSON-8 (NGQ0008A, p45–p47):**
  - Body 3.0 × 3.0 mm (2.9–3.1), 0.5 mm pitch, 0.8 mm max height.
  - Exposed pad 1.6 ± 0.1 × 2.0 ± 0.1 mm, tied to $V^-$ inside. It must be soldered, and the copper attached to it must be at $V^-$ (p19, p45 note 3).
  - $\theta_{JA}$ = 71 °C/W (p4); MSL 3.
  - Pins: 1 $\overline{PD}$ (floats high = on), 2 FB (internally tied to OUT), 3 IN−, 4 IN+, 5 $V^-$, 6 COMP (pull-down), 7 OUT, 8 $V^+$.
  - Orderables SD/SDE/SDX (/NOPB), marking L6629 (p38).
- **SOT-23-5 (DBV0005A, p42–p44):**
  - 2.90 × 1.60 mm nominal body, 0.95 mm pitch, 1.45 mm max height, no exposed pad.
  - $\theta_{JA}$ = 179 °C/W; MSL 1.
  - Pins: 1 OUT, 2 $V^-$, 3 +IN, 4 −IN, 5 $V^+$. No PD, COMP or FB.
  - Orderables MF/MFE/MFX, marking AE7A.
- **Logic inputs ($\overline{PD}$, COMP):** $V_{IL}$ ≤ 0.8 V; $V_{IH}$ ≥ 2.5 V (5 V) or ≥ 2.0 V (3.3 V). Input current ≈ −28 / −22 µA (flows out) (p7, p10).
- **Layout (p34–p36):**
  - Low-inductance ceramic decoupling < 0.5 inch from the pins: 1000 pF–0.1 µF chip capacitors plus 4.7–10 µF tantalum.
  - HD2 is strongly affected by layout, in particular by decoupling capacitors between $V^+$ and $V^-$ placed close to the leads.
  - Remove the ground plane near the inputs; keep $R_F$/$R_G$ traces short; terminate traces longer than 1 inch.
  - The FB pin lets $R_F$ sit next to IN−.
  - Bias-current cancellation ($R_b \ge 25$ Ω, optionally shunted by a capacitor). Use at least 25 Ω in series with IN+ when it is driven from a 0 Ω source (p23).
  - Capacitive loads need a series $R_{ISO}$ (Figs 13, 14).

## 12. Uncertain or inconsistent items

1. GBWP (4 GHz / 1.7 GHz) appears only in the application text, not in the specification tables. Fig 31 is consistent with it (3.8 / 1.7 GHz).
2. Output headroom is stated as 0.8 V (p1) and as ~0.85 V (p33); the table typical is 0.81–0.82 V into 100 Ω at 5 V.
3. $I_B$ sign and magnitude: the table gives −15 µA TYP, while Fig 46 shows +12 µA. This is a magnitude plot of a typical unit.
4. Fig 17's 240 Ω trace matches Fig 15's trace sample-for-sample, even though the conditions differ (±1.5 V vs ±2.5 V). This suggests reused data.
5. Fig 51: the text says "about 1 GHz", but the measured curve reads −3 dB at 1.22 GHz (0 dB re-crossing at 1.02 GHz).
6. The TIA bandwidth "around 200 MHz" is a calculation; there is no measured TIA response. The TIA noise plot ignores $e_n$ × capacitance noise gain.
7. The trace-to-label assignment within the Fig 13 capacitive-load sweep and the Figs 15–18 $R_f$ sweeps (750 Ω vs 1 kΩ) is not certain.
