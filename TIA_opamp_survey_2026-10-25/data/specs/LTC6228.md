# LTC6228 — spec sheet extract

**Source:** `datasheets/ltc6228_distributor_copy.pdf` = LTC6228/LTC6229 data sheet **Rev. B (03/20)**, © Analog Devices 2019–2020 (Rev. A 09/19 added LTC6229; Rev. B corrected values on pp 1, 9, 17, 20, 30). Production data sheet. PDF page index = printed page number ("pN").

Types: TYP / MIN / MAX = 25 °C table values ("over T" = bullet rows); CURVE = read from the PDF vector paths (±1–3 %) or by eye where marked "visual".

## Architecture
- **Bipolar input** (PNP pair) with optional input-bias-current cancellation; rail-to-rail output; **unity-gain stable** (p1, p17).
- Supply 2.8–11.75 V total (abs max 12 V); specified at ±5 V, 5 V, 3 V. I_Q = 16 mA typ / 16.9 mA max per channel (±5 V); shutdown 500 µA.

## Noise (G13, p11, ±5 V; vector data)
- $e_n$ = 0.88 nV/√Hz at 1 MHz (TYP); curve: 2.64 at 1 kHz, 1.29 at 10 kHz, 0.93 at 100 kHz, 0.88 at 1 MHz, 0.98 at 10 MHz, 1.26 at 20 MHz, 1.43 at 30 MHz, 1.26 at 50 MHz (plot ends ≈50 MHz).
- $i_n$ = 3 pA/√Hz at 1 MHz (bias cancellation disabled), 6.3 pA/√Hz (enabled) (TYP). Curve, cancellation disabled: 4.1 at 100 kHz, 3.0 at 1 MHz, 2.45 at 3 MHz, 2.2 at 10 MHz, 2.9 at 20 MHz, 3.2 at 30 MHz (plot ends ≈30 MHz; mild upturn above 10 MHz, no explanation given). Strong low-frequency excess (49 pA/√Hz at 100 Hz).
- Text (p18, p20): lower $e_n$ is bought with higher collector current, raising $i_n$; enabling bias cancellation raises current noise; with $e_n$ = 0.88 nV/√Hz and $i_n$ = 3 pA/√Hz, current noise dominates when $R_{EQ}$ > 1.8 kΩ.

## Input
- $C_{IN}$: **differential 3.5 pF, common mode 1.5 pF** (TYP, no frequency range stated); $R_{IN}$ 2.6 kΩ differential, 4 MΩ common mode (p3).
- $I_B$ ≈ −16 µA typ (cancellation off); 0.6 µA typ, 2.5 µA max with cancellation (p3). $V_{OS}$ 20 µV typ, 95 µV max (250 µV over T); drift 0.4 µV/°C.
- Input CM range V− − 0.1 V to V+ − 1.2 V; differential input clamped at ±0.7 V by back-to-back diodes (limit current < 10 mA).

## TIA content
- p30 typical application: photodiode SFH213 with a JFET (2SK932-22) source-follower front end inside the LTC6228 loop, R_F = 1 MΩ, C_F = 0.055 pF parasitic, ±5 V: −3 dB 4.6 MHz, rise 77 ns, 661 µV rms integrated output noise (measured). Not directly comparable with a 2 kΩ, 150–200 MHz TIA.
- Feedback-pole guidance (p18–20): 1 kΩ/1 kΩ at G = +2 with 7 pF at −IN oscillates (pole 45 MHz) — fix with 7 pF across R_F; 5 kΩ with 400 fF parasitic affects gain above 79 MHz.

## Packages
- S8 SOIC-8 (θ_JA 120 °C/W; pin 1 = FB, tied to OUT internally; pins 4 and 5 both V−), S6 TSOT-23 (192 °C/W), DC6 2 × 2 mm DFN, 0.5 mm pitch, exposed pad (≈1.37 × 0.60 mm) = V−, must be soldered (80 °C/W). Dual LTC6229: DD10 3 × 3 (43 °C/W), MS8E (35 °C/W).
