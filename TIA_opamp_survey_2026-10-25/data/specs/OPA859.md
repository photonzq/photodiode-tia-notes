Value types:
- TYP and MIN/MAX are table values.
- CURVE values come from sampling the plot's vector path.
- SIM means the datasheet says the result is simulated or calculated.

## Noise

**Voltage noise.** Fig 6-11 (CURVE): 32.2 nV/rtHz at 1 kHz, 4.60 at 100 kHz, 3.41 at 1 MHz, 3.27 at 10 MHz, 3.25 at 50 MHz. The highest plotted frequency is about 100 MHz. The 1/f corner is about 120 kHz (estimated from the curve). At 10 MHz the noise rises with temperature: 2.79 nV/rtHz at -40 C, 3.21 at 25 C, 3.99 at 125 C.

**Current noise.** Fig 8-8 is identical to the OPA858 curve. Values: 53 fA/rtHz at 1 MHz, 0.52 pA at 10 MHz, 4.65 pA at 100 MHz, 18.1 pA at 500 MHz. It rises about +20 dB/decade, proportional to f. The text attributes the rise to capacitive coupling between the gate oxide and the channel, as in the OPA858 datasheet. It does not say whether the curve is measured or simulated.

## Uncertain or contradictory items

1. The p22 text says R_F = 10/20 kOhm and C_PD = 1/2 pF, but the Fig 9-2 and 9-3 legends show 2/5 kOhm and 0.5/1 pF.
2. The text gives a 63 deg buffer phase margin; Fig 6-10 reads about 75 deg.
3. The open-loop magnitude rises again above 4 GHz.
4. HD values are printed without a sign.
5. Figs 8-7 and 8-8 are copied from the OPA858 datasheet, and the current-noise curve's origin is not stated.
