# OPA955 — specification sheet (TIA survey)

**Datasheet:** `datasheets/opa955.pdf`, Texas Instruments SLOSEE6, July 2026 (initial release, revision "*"). Full record: `OPA955.json`.

## Status: ADVANCE INFORMATION — every value is preliminary
- Every datasheet page (p1–p17) carries a vertical "ADVANCE INFORMATION" banner. The p1 footer reads: "ADVANCE INFORMATION for preproduction products; subject to change without notice."
- The only orderable part, **XOPA955DSGR**, is listed as Active with material type **Preproduction**. Preproduction means prototype or experimental, not released for full production, sold without warranty, and purchasable only with a waiver. RoHS is shown as "-"; MSL and lead finish are "Call TI" (p18).
- No individual parameter is singled out as preliminary, and nothing is labelled as simulated. All table values and curves should therefore be treated as preliminary.
- Several numbers are identical to the OPA855 datasheet and may simply be carried over:
  - all thermal metrics;
  - $V_{OS}$ limits, $I_B$ drift and $I_{OS}$ drift;
  - output-swing limits;
  - ESD ratings and supply ranges;
  - common-mode input resistance.

## How to read this sheet
- **Citations:** "p5 EC" means PDF page 5, which is also printed page 5. PDF and printed numbers match for pages 1–17; p18 is the package addendum, p19 the generic package view, p20 the legal notice.
- ***EC* conditions** (Section 6.5, p5–p6): $V_{S+}=5$ V, $V_{S-}=0$, $G=7$ V/V, $R_F=453\ \Omega$, input common mode at midsupply, $R_L=200\ \Omega$ to midsupply, $T_A=25$ °C. There are no over-temperature MIN/MAX limits.
- ***TC* conditions** (Section 6.6, p7–p8): $T_A=25$ °C, ±2.5 V supplies, $R_F=453\ \Omega$, $G=7$ V/V, $R_L=200\ \Omega$.

## 4. Noise (Fig 6-7, p8, TC conditions; vector data; plotted to about 0.95 GHz)
| f | 1 kHz | 10 kHz | 100 kHz | 1 MHz | 10 MHz | 50 MHz | 100 MHz | 200 MHz | 300 MHz | 500 MHz | 900 MHz |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| $e_n$ (nV/√Hz) | 1.56 | 0.883 | 0.793 | 0.807 | 0.807 | 0.807 | 0.806 | 0.806 | 0.805 | 0.802 | 0.797 |
| $i_n$ (pA/√Hz) | 0.717 | 0.573 | 0.557 | 0.557 | 0.603 | 0.764 | 1.10 | 1.88 | 2.67 | 4.25 | 8.92 |

- **Spot values:** $e_n$ = 0.79 nV/√Hz and $i_n$ = 0.55 pA/√Hz at 1 MHz (TYP, p5 EC; the table prints the current-noise symbol as $e_i$).
- **1/f corner (DERIVED):** about 2.5 kHz for $e_n$.
- **High-frequency current noise rises:** it starts rising slowly above about 2 MHz and grows roughly in proportion to $f$ above about 100 MHz, reaching 9.8 pA/√Hz at 0.95 GHz.
- **How the current noise was measured is not stated.**
- **DERIVED observation:** the excess above the 0.56 pA/√Hz floor matches $e_n\cdot2\pi f\cdot C$ with $C\approx1.9$ pF. If the curve already contains this voltage-noise-on-input-capacitance term, adding $e_n\omega C_{in}$ again in a TIA noise model would count it twice.

## 8. TIA material in the datasheet
The datasheet does not say whether either front-page plot is measured or simulated.

1. **"Transimpedance Bandwidth vs Frequency" (p1).** $R_F$ = 10 kΩ, $C_F$ = 0.1 pF, $C_{IN}$ = 4.7 pF, $I_{IN}$ = 20 µApp. The datasheet does not say whether $C_{IN}$ includes the amplifier's own input capacitance.
   - Reading (CURVE): −3 dB at about **112 MHz**, with no peaking.
   - −0.5 dB at 50 MHz, −2.4 dB at 100 MHz, −8.8 dB at 200 MHz, −14.9 dB at 300 MHz.
2. **"Transimpedance Pulse Response" (p1).** Same $R_F$, $C_F$ and $C_{IN}$, driven by a 100 µA input pulse.
   - Output step about 1.03 V.
   - 10–90 % rise about **3.1 ns**; fall about 3.0 ns.
   - 50 % width about 9.8 ns.
   - Undershoot about 20 mV after the pulse.
   - Raster reading, about ±0.2 ns.
3. **TIA layout guidance (p14–p15).**
   - If $C_F$ is below 0.15 pF, use two series resistors of $R_F/2$.
   - Place $R_{ISO}$ of 10–20 Ω right at IN−.
   - Keep the APD close and on the same side of the board.
   - Close the loop formed by $R_F$, $C_F$ and $R_{ISO}$ at the APD pins.
4. **No other TIA material:** no bandwidth-versus-$R_F$ or bandwidth-versus-$C_D$ tables or plots, and no TIA design example.

## 9. Package, pinout, layout
**Pinout** (p3; same as OPA855)
- 1 FB (internally connected to the output), 2 NC (leave floating), 3 IN−, 4 IN+, 5 VS−, 6 OUT, 7 VS+, 8 PD (low = power off).
- $R_F$ fits between FB and IN− on the same side of the package (p11 Fig 8-4).

**Thermal pad connection** (stated three ways)
- Table 5-1 (p3): "a heat-spreading power or ground plane".
- §8.4.1 (p12): the negative supply in split-supply use, ground in single-supply use.
- Fig 9-2 (p14): "negative supply pin".

**DSG (WSON-8)**
- 2 × 2 mm, 0.5 mm pitch, 0.8 mm maximum height (generic view, p19).
- This revision has **no outline, land-pattern or stencil drawing**.

**Thermal** (p4; identical to OPA855)
- $R_{\theta JA}$ 80.1 °C/W; $R_{\theta JC(top)}$ 100; $R_{\theta JB}$ 45; $\Psi_{JT}$ 6.8; $\Psi_{JB}$ 45.2; $R_{\theta JC(bot)}$ 22.7.

**Layout** (p14): same guidance as OPA855 — cut the planes under the I/O pins, bypass capacitors within 0.25 in, tie PD to VS+.

## 10. Absolute maximum, protection, power down
- **Absolute maximum** (p4): $V_S$ 5.5 V; inputs from $V_{S-}-0.5$ V to $V_{S+}+0.5$ V; $V_{ID}$ 1 V; input current ±10 mA; output current ±50 mA; $T_J$ 150 °C.
- **ESD** (p4): HBM ±1500 V, CDM ±1500 V.
- **Input protection** (p11 Fig 8-3): anti-parallel diodes between the inputs.
- **Power down** (p6): the reference node is not stated.
  - Disable ("off below"): 0.65 V MIN, 0.95 V TYP. Enable ("on above"): 1 V TYP, 1.8 V MAX.
  - PD pin bias current 30 µA TYP (50 µA MAX).
  - Turn-on 120 ns, turn-off 30 ns.
  - Power-down supply current 465 µA TYP (520 µA MAX).

## 11. Inconsistencies and uncertainties found
1. $A_{OL}$: 76 dB TYP in the table against about 87 dB on Fig 6-6.
2. $i_n$ = 0.55 pA/√Hz is below the full shot noise of the specified typical bias current: $\sqrt{2qI_B}$ = 0.80 pA/√Hz at 2 µA. That is unusual for a bipolar input, so either the noise or the bias-current value may be preliminary or in error.
3. The Fig 6-6 phase starts at +12° at 100 kHz.
4. Fig 6-1, G = −7: the curve shows about 4 dB of peaking; the text on p9 says 5 dB.
5. The thermal-pad connection is worded differently in three places (Section 9 above).
6. The thermal table and several DC limits are identical to OPA855's.
7. $V_{IL}$ at 3.3 V is 0.9 V TYP (lower than 1.1 V at 5 V) but 1.4 V MAX (higher than 1.35 V at 5 V).
8. Typos: "Bandwdith", "respone", and the current-noise symbol $e_i$.
9. Current-noise rise above 100 MHz: the measurement definition is missing, so it is unclear whether the curve includes $e_n\cdot\omega C_{in}$.
