## Other notes, inconsistencies and uncertainties

- Two characterizations in one datasheet: THS4021D/THS4022DGN (p6-p7, Figs 5-1..5-31) vs THS4021DGN (p8-p9, Figs 5-32..5-56) with materially different numbers (Vn 1.2 vs 1.5 nV/rtHz; In 2.3 vs 2 pA/rtHz; IB 9 vs 3 uA; AOL 100 dB vs 60 V/mV; SSBW G=10 290 vs 350 MHz; RO 5 vs 13 Ohm; IO 200 mA at 10 Ohm vs 100 mA at 20 Ohm).
- DGN noise curve (Fig 5-54) shows ~1.13-1.33 nV/rtHz and ~1.2-1.4 pA/rtHz at 10-100 kHz, below the DGN EC values (1.5 nV, 2 pA). D current-noise curve (Fig 5-12) shows ~2.1 pA/rtHz vs 2.3 in the EC.
- DGN full-power bandwidth: 3.7 MHz (20 Vpp) and 11.8 MHz (5 Vpp) are half of SR/(2 pi VO(peak)) = 7.5 and 23.6 MHz computed with the listed slew rates; the D table (7.5, 23.6 MHz) is self-consistent with its note SR/(pi VO(PP)).
- DGN EC (p8) THD and DG/DP conditions printed as 'gain = 2' although the part is stated stable only for gains >= 10 (D table uses gain = 10).
- DGN output-swing rows 3-4 (p9) are printed with RL = 150 Ohm but give larger swing (+/-13.5, +/-3.8 V) than the RL = 250 / 150 Ohm rows; they match the D table's RL = 1 kOhm rows.
- Fig 5-12 (current noise) caption lists 'RL = 150 Ohm, VOUT = 2 VPP' (copied plot conditions).
- Fig 5-27 is captioned 'Input Offset Current vs Ambient Temperature' but shows a distribution histogram (mean 0.164, sigma 0.0395).
- Device Information table (p1) lists THS4022 only in DGN, while the addendum lists active THS4022 SOIC (D) orderables.
- Open-loop phase reaches -180 deg (~320-360 MHz) while |AOL| ~ +11 dB, i.e. not unity-gain stable; capacitive TIA feedback reduces the high-frequency noise gain toward 1 + C_in/C_F and must keep it >= ~10 at the loop crossover (consequence of the stated 10 V/V minimum gain; not discussed in the datasheet).
