# OPA855 — specification sheet (TIA survey)

**Datasheet:** `datasheets/opa855.pdf`, Texas Instruments SBOS622D (July 2018, revised May 2025). **Status: Production Data** (p1 footer; revision A changed Advance Information to Production Data; revision D moved the bare die from preview to production). Full record: `OPA855.json`.

**Citation format:** "p6 EC" means PDF page 6, which is also printed page 6 (pages 1–32 carry identical PDF and printed numbers; pages 33–41 are the addendum and mechanical drawings).

**Condition sets**
- *EC* (Section 6.5, p6–p7): $V_{S+}=5$ V, $V_{S-}=0$, $G=7$ V/V, $R_F=453\ \Omega$, input common mode at midsupply, $R_L=200\ \Omega$ to midsupply, $T_A=25$ °C. Footnote 1 says MIN/MAX limits do not apply to the bare die.
- *TC* (Section 6.6 typical characteristics, p8–p14): $T_A=25$ °C, $\pm2.5$ V supplies, $V_{IN+}=0$, $R_F=453\ \Omega$, $G=7$ V/V, $R_L=200\ \Omega$.

**Value types**
- TYP: typical value.
- MIN/MAX: guaranteed limits at 25 °C (the table has no over-temperature MIN/MAX).
- CURVE: read from a plot. Vector-path data have an uncertainty of about ±0.2 dB or ±1°; raster readings carry the uncertainty quoted with them.
- CALC: the datasheet says the values were computed.
- DERIVED: calculated here from datasheet data; the datasheet does not state it.

**Operation below the minimum gain**
- p25 states that the part "requires a high-frequency gain of 7 V/V or greater to be stable"; lower gains give increased peaking and possible instability.
- p18 states that phase margin is below 0° at a gain of 1.
- Example (p15 §7): the inverting −7 V/V test circuit has a noise gain of 5.3 V/V and shows 5 dB of peaking.
- TIA compensation: the high-frequency noise gain is $1+C_{TOT}/C_F$ (Eq. 2, p25), where $C_{TOT}$ is the photodiode capacitance plus the common-mode and differential input capacitance. A decompensated amplifier suits a TIA because this noise gain exceeds 1 (p18).

## 8. TIA material in the datasheet
1. **Fig 9-2 (also the front-page plot), p23.** Bandwidth and integrated input noise $I_{RN}$ (noise bandwidth = $f_{-3dB}$) versus photodiode capacitance, for $R_F$ = 6 kΩ and 12 kΩ. Type: **CALC** — the values were computed with TI's TIA application-report equations and Excel calculator (p22). $C_F$ is not given.
   - $R_F$ = 6 kΩ, bandwidth: 404 MHz at 0.5 pF, 343.5 at 1 pF, 303.8 at 1.5 pF, 275.3 at 2 pF, 236.4 at 3 pF, 191.3 at 5 pF, 140.2 at 10 pF, 101 at 20 pF.
   - $R_F$ = 12 kΩ, bandwidth: 285.6 MHz at 0.5 pF, 242.9 at 1 pF, 214.8 at 1.5 pF, 194.7 at 2 pF, 135.3 at 5 pF, 99.2 at 10 pF, 71.6 at 20 pF.
   - $I_{RN}$: 69.0 nA rms ($R_F$ = 6 kΩ) and 49.4 nA rms (12 kΩ) at 1 pF.
2. **Fig 9-3, p23 (CALC).** Bandwidth and $I_{RN}$ versus $R_F$ from 5 to 20 kΩ, for $C_{PD}$ = 1.5 pF and 2.5 pF. The legend mislabels $C_{PD}$ as $C_F$.
   - 1.5 pF: 332 MHz at 5 kΩ, 235 at 10 kΩ, 168 at 20 kΩ.
   - 2.5 pF: 277 MHz at 5 kΩ, 196.5 at 10 kΩ, 140 at 20 kΩ.
   - $I_{RN}$ (1.5 pF): 52.3 nA rms at 10 kΩ, 38.9 at 20 kΩ.
3. **Figs 9-8 to 9-10, p27.** TIA response for $C_D$ = 1 to 500 pF with $R_F$ = 1, 10 and 100 kΩ. $C_F$ is not stated, and the datasheet does not say whether these are measured or simulated; the bitmaps look like simulator output.
   - $C_D$ = 1 pF, $R_F$ = 1 kΩ: about +0.7 dB peak near 0.6 GHz, −3 dB at about 1.09 GHz (CURVE). The text on p26 gives 1.1 GHz.
   - $R_F$ = 1 kΩ, $C_D$ = 10 pF: about 370 MHz; $C_D$ = 50 pF: about 160 MHz.
4. **Table 9-1 and Fig 9-7, p25 / p27.** Design parameters per detector type:

   | Detector | Capacitance | $R_F$ | Target bandwidth |
   |---|---|---|---|
   | PIN photodiode | 3 pF | 100 kΩ | 15 MHz |
   | APD | 1 pF | 10 kΩ | 200 MHz |
   | PMT | 50 pF | 1 kΩ | 100 MHz |
   | MPPC (SiPM) | 100 pF | 1 kΩ | 10 MHz |

   Fig 9-7 plots the corresponding responses (CURVE, ±5 %). The −3 dB points are about 15 MHz (PIN), 225 MHz (APD), 77 MHz (PMT) and 3.8 MHz (MPPC), so the PMT and MPPC curves fall short of their targets. $C_F$ is not stated.
5. **Fig 9-1, p21–p22.** Signal chain: APD TIA → THS4520 fully differential amplifier → ADS54J64 ADC. The TIA common mode is 3.8 V on a 5 V supply (1.2 V below the positive rail) to maximize negative swing. No $R_F$ or $C_F$ values are given.
6. **TIA layout (p29–p30).**
   - If the required $C_F$ is below 0.15 pF, build $R_F$ from two series resistors of $R_F/2$.
   - Put an $R_{ISO}$ of 10–20 Ω right at IN−.
   - Close the loop formed by $R_F$, $C_F$ and $R_{ISO}$ at the APD pins.
   - Keep the photodiode on the same side of the board as the amplifier.
   - Noise gain is $1+Z_F/Z_{IN}$ (Eq. 3).

## 9. Package, pinout, layout
**Pinout**
- 1 FB (internally connected to the output), 2 NC (leave floating), 3 IN−, 4 IN+, 5 VS−, 6 OUT, 7 VS+, 8 PD (low = power off). The thermal pad connects to VS− (p3 Table 5-1).
- $R_F$ fits between pins 1 and 3 on the same side of the package (p17 Fig 8-4).

**DSG (WSON-8), drawing DSG0008A**
- Body 2 × 2 mm (1.9–2.1 mm), 0.5 mm pitch, 0.7–0.8 mm height.
- Exposed pad 0.9 ± 0.1 × 1.6 ± 0.1 mm; it must be soldered.
- Land pattern: 0.5 × 0.25 mm pads, 1.9 mm row spacing, two Ø0.2 mm vias in the thermal land.
- Sources: p37–p40.

**Thermal** (p5)
- $R_{\theta JA}$ 80.1 °C/W; $R_{\theta JC(top)}$ 100; $R_{\theta JB}$ 45; $\Psi_{JT}$ 6.8; $\Psi_{JB}$ 45.2; $R_{\theta JC(bot)}$ 22.7 °C/W.

**Bare die (OPA855YR)**
- 0.751 × 0.705 mm, 381 µm thick; backside connected to VS−; pad map and coordinates on p4.

**Orderables** (p33–p34)
- OPA855IDSGR (reel of 3000) and OPA855IDSGT (250), marking 855, MSL 1.
- An automotive OPA855-Q1 exists.

**Layout guidance** (p29 §9.4.1)
- Cut the ground and power planes under the I/O pins.
- Place bypass capacitors within 0.25 in.
- Tie PD to VS+ to enable the amplifier.

## 10. Absolute maximum, protection, power down
- **Absolute maximum** (p5): $V_S$ 5.5 V; inputs from $V_{S-}-0.5$ V to $V_{S+}+0.5$ V; $V_{ID}$ 1 V; input current ±10 mA; $T_J$ 150 °C.
- **ESD** (p5): HBM ±1500 V, CDM ±1500 V.
- **Input protection** (p17 Fig 8-3): two anti-parallel diodes between the inputs, plus diodes to both supplies.
- **Power down** (p7, p20): thresholds are referenced to VS−.
  - Disable: 0.65 V MIN, 1 V TYP. Enable: 1.5 V TYP, 1.8 V MAX.
  - Power-down supply current 70 µA TYP (140 µA MAX). Turn-on 15 ns, turn-off 120 ns.
  - In power down, a differential input larger than one diode drop opens a low-impedance path from IN+ to OUT.

## 11. Inconsistencies and uncertainties found
1. Phase margin: 62° in the text (p18) against about 74° derived from Fig 6-10.
2. $A_{OL}$: 76 dB TYP in the table against about 80 dB on Fig 6-10 and Fig 8-5.
3. Small-signal bandwidth: 2.5 GHz in the table against about 3.1 GHz on Fig 6-1 (which uses ±2.5 V supplies).
4. Large-signal bandwidth: 850 MHz in the table against about 0.95 GHz on Figs 6-6 and 6-8.
5. 0.1-dB flatness: 200 MHz in the table against about 440 MHz on Fig 6-7.
6. Current noise: 2.5 pA/√Hz in the table against 2.84 pA/√Hz on the curve at 1 MHz. Voltage noise at 10 MHz and 25 °C: 0.965 (Fig 6-12), 0.99 (Fig 6-11), 0.98 (table).
7. Labelling errors:
   - Fig 9-3 legend says $C_F$ where the text says $C_{PD}$, and has a stray "$I_{RN}$, $R_F$ = 2.5 pF" entry.
   - Figs 6-15 and 6-16 legends read "VOUT = 100 Ω".
   - Figs 6-37 and 6-38 label the y-axis in mV although the values are volts.
   - p22 says "ADC54J64" (should be ADS54J64).
8. Fig 6-1, G = −7: the text quotes 5 dB of peaking; the curve reads about 4.3 dB.
9. The TIA bandwidth and noise curves of Figs 9-2 and 9-3 are calculated, not measured. Figs 9-7 to 9-10 give no $C_F$ and no statement of method.
