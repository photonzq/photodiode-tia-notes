**Open-loop gain/phase** (Fig. 6-20, p11, CURVE ±0.2 dB/±0.5°): 97.7 dB/−3° (1 kHz), 73.5/−80 (100 kHz), 53.5/−89 (1 MHz), 33.5/−91 (10 MHz), 23.9/−92 (30 MHz), 13.5/−96 (100 MHz), 7.6/−103 (200 MHz), 4.3/−110 (300 MHz), 0.4/−124 (500 MHz), −2.1/−138 (700 MHz), −3.8/−151 (900 MHz). 0 dB at 528 MHz → unity-gain PM ≈ 54° (DERIVED).

**Noise curves** (p10): $e_n$ (Fig. 6-16, 10 Hz–1 MHz): 23.4 nV/√Hz @100 Hz, 8.5 @1 kHz, 5.05 @10 kHz, 4.56 @100 kHz, 4.50 @1 MHz. $i_n$ (Fig. 6-17, 10 kHz–100 MHz, no simulation label): 1.4 fA/√Hz @10 kHz, 11 @100 kHz, 109 @1 MHz, 1.07 pA/√Hz @10 MHz, 3.0 @30 MHz, 4.5 @50 MHz, ~5.35 pA/√Hz near 100 MHz — ∝ $f$ up to ~30 MHz, flattening above ~60 MHz. DERIVED: $i_n/(e_n 2\pi f)$ = 3.8 pF at 1 and 10 MHz (fixed-capacitance behaviour).

**Swing vs current** (Fig. 6-24, 25 °C): +3.81/−3.82 V @0.5 mA, +3.55/−3.52 V @50 mA; sinking side collapses above ~60 mA (−2.8 V @70 mA).

Uncertain/contradictory: $i_n$ 18 fA/√Hz has no frequency (curve passes 18 fA/√Hz near 165 kHz); package called WSON but the outline page (p27) is titled WQFN; Fig. 7-6 temperature legend not re-read.
