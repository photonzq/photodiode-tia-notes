**Datasheet:** `datasheets/ad8021_distributor_copy.pdf`, Analog Devices AD8021 **Rev. F** (5/06; footer code C01888-0-5/06(F)). Production data sheet. Full record: `AD8021.json`.

**Citation format:** "PDF 5 / p4" = PDF page 5, printed page 4. The distributor copy has an extra "Important Links" page at PDF 2, so printed page = PDF page − 1 from PDF 3 on.

**Value types:** TYP; MIN/MAX (guaranteed at 25 °C; no over-temperature limits in the tables); CURVE (vector-path digitization, ±0.2 dB / ±1°); DERIVED (computed here).

$G_N \times$ BW uses the peaked (≤ 1 dB) closed-loop bandwidth and is not a true GBW.

**Fig. 59 (PDF 20 / p19), $C_C$ for 1 dB peaking vs noise gain (CURVE, ±0.1 pF):** $G_N$ 1 → 10 pF; 1.5 → 8.4; 2 → 6.9; 2.5 → 5.9; 3 → 4.9; 4 → 3.1; 5 → 2.1; 6 → 1.5; 7 → 0.95; 8 → 0.54; 9 → 0.22; 10 → ≈ 0 pF.

Fig. 58 (simplified, instructional): $C_C$ = 10 pF at G = +1 gives ≈ 200 MHz and ≈ 60°; the same $C_C$ at G = +10 gives ≈ 20 MHz and ≈ 90°; $C_C$ = 0 at G = +10 returns ≈ 200 MHz and ≈ 60°. Slew rate scales roughly as $1/C_C$.

**Below the recommended $C_C$ / TIA notes:** lower $C_C$ raises bandwidth and peaking; higher $C_C$ raises phase margin. A $C_F$ across $R_F$ compensates the pole formed by $R_F$ and the input capacitance (Fig. 14: $R_F$ = 1 kΩ, $C_F$ = 2.2 pF at G = +2). The data sheet has no TIA example.

Pinout: 1 LOGIC REFERENCE, 2 −IN, 3 +IN, 4 −V$_S$, 5 $C_{COMP}$, 6 V$_{OUT}$, 7 +V$_S$, 8 $\overline{DISABLE}$. Leaving pins 1 and 8 open keeps the part enabled.

**Layout (PDF 22 / p21, Fig. 62):** guard ring around pin 5 tied to the output (pin 6); $C_C$ as a COG/NPO SMD capacitor right at the package between pins 4 and 5 (no leaded parts); bypass at pins 4 and 7; $R_F$ under the board from pin 6 to pin 2; $R_G$ return next to the pin-4 bypass.

## 8. Protection and abs max
Differential input ±0.8 V and ±10 mA (input diodes, no series resistors, to keep noise low); common-mode input ±V$_S$ ± 1 V; operating −40 to +85 °C; $T_J$ 150 °C (PDF 8 / p7).

## 9. Caveats
- Differential input capacitance is not specified (only 1 pF common mode).
- Noise is characterized only to 10 MHz.
- Fig. 37 trace (2.35–2.4 nV/√Hz) disagrees with its own 2.1 nV/√Hz label.
- Fig. 58 text (≈ 200 MHz at G = +1, $C_C$ = 10 pF) disagrees with Table 1 (490 MHz); Fig. 58 is labelled simplified.
- Skipped pages: PDF 2 (distributor links), PDF 3 (contents and revision history), PDF 27–29 (blank notes pages).
