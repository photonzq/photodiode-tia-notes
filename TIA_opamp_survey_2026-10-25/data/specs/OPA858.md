# OPA858 (Texas Instruments) - spec sheet

Source: `datasheets/opa858.pdf`, SBOS629B (April 2018, revised May 2025), PRODUCTION DATA. PDF page index equals printed page number for pp. 1-28.

Types used below:
- TYP and MIN/MAX are table values.
- CURVE values come from sampling the plot's vector path.
- SIM means the datasheet calls the result simulated or calculated.

Conditions:
- EC table (p6-7): VS = 5 V single, G = 7, RF = 453 ohm, VCM at midsupply, RL = 200 ohm to midsupply, 25 C.
- Typical plots (p8-14): +/-2.5 V, same RF, G and RL.

## Noise

- **Voltage noise (Fig 6-11, p9, CURVE)**: 25.2 nV/rtHz at 1 kHz, 8.5 at 10 kHz, 3.57 at 100 kHz, 2.62 at 1 MHz, 2.50 at 10 MHz, 2.45 at 96 MHz. The highest frequency plotted is 100 MHz. The 1/f corner estimated from the curve is about 110 kHz; the datasheet does not state it. At 10 MHz the noise varies with temperature (Fig 6-12): 1.98 nV/rtHz at -40 C, 2.50 at 25 C, 3.18 at 125 C.
- **Current noise (Fig 8-8, p19, CURVE)**: one curve covers both inputs (I_BN and I_BI). Values per rtHz: 0.51 fA at 1 kHz, 6.2 fA at 100 kHz, 53 fA at 1 MHz, 0.52 pA at 10 MHz, 1.0 pA at 20 MHz, 2.39 pA at 50 MHz, 4.65 pA at 100 MHz, 8.9 pA at 200 MHz, 18.1 pA at 500 MHz, 25.5 pA at 960 MHz. The curve rises about +20 dB/decade (proportional to f) from 1 to 100 MHz, then eases to about +15 dB/decade.
- **Why it rises (p19, 8.3.5)**: at low frequency the gate impedance is several GOhm, so there is no bias-current shot noise. At high frequency, capacitive coupling between the CMOS gate oxide and the channel raises the current noise, which the datasheet calls "a natural artifact of the construction of the transistor and is unavoidable". The datasheet does not say whether the curve is measured or simulated.

## TIA content

- **Figs 9-2 / 9-3 and p1 (SIM, calculated with the TI TIA calculator)**: the noise bandwidth is set equal to f-3dB. C_F is not stated.

  | C_PD | f-3dB, R_F = 10 k | f-3dB, R_F = 20 k |
  |---|---|---|
  | 1 pF | 220.6 MHz | 155.9 MHz |
  | 2 pF | 176.8 MHz | 125.0 MHz |
  | 5 pF | 122.9 MHz | 86.8 MHz |
  | 10 pF | 90.0 MHz | 63.7 MHz |
  | 20 pF | 65.1 MHz | 46.1 MHz |

  Integrated input noise is 56.9 nA rms at 10 k and 1 pF, and 33.8 nA rms at 20 k and 1 pF.

  Versus R_F at 1 pF: 310 MHz at 5 k, 98.7 MHz at 50 k, 70.1 MHz at 99 k. The Fig 9-3 legend says "C_F" but means C_PD.
- **Fig 9-1 (APD TIA)**: the APD cathode is at +100 V. IN+ is biased at 3.4 V on a 5 V supply (1.6 V below the rail) so the output can swing negative.
- **Layout**:
  - If C_F would need to be below 0.15 pF, use two series R_F/2 resistors.
  - Put the APD on the same side of the board as the amplifier.
  - Add R_ISO = 10-20 ohm at IN-.
  - Close the R_F/C_F/R_ISO loop at the APD pins (p26-27).
- **FB pin**: pin 1 is the output and pin 2 is an isolating NC. R_F goes from FB to IN- (p17).

## Uncertain or contradictory items

1. Fig 6-10 versus Fig 8-5 and the table GBWP (see Open loop).
2. The Fig 9-3 legend says C_F where C_PD is meant.
3. HD values are printed without a sign, and the 10 MHz HD2 in the table differs from the curve by 11 dB.
4. The 0.1 dB flatness is 130 MHz in the table but about 197 MHz on the curve.
5. The bias current is 10 pA on p1 but 0.4 pA typ / 5 pA max in the table.
6. The current-noise curve's origin (measured or simulated) is not stated. It is identical to the OPA859 curve.
