# LMH32401: integrated differential-output TIA with ambient-light cancellation (Texas Instruments)

**Datasheet:** `datasheets/lmh32401.pdf`, SBOS965D (October 2019, revised January 2023), Production Data. The package addendum is dated 12 May 2026; the VQFN and bare-die parts are both listed as Active. An automotive variant, LMH32401-Q1, also exists.

**Citations:** pX is the PDF page index. For p1–p31 it equals the printed page number.

**Value types:**
- TYP / MIN / MAX: values from the Electrical Characteristics (EC) tables.
- CURVE: read from a 300-dpi clip of a typical-characteristic plot.
- DERIVED: computed by me from datasheet values.
- TEXT: taken from the datasheet's prose.

**Default EC and typical-curve conditions (p6–p11):**
- $V_{DD}$ = 3.3 V, VOCM open, VOD = 0 V.
- $C_{PD}$ = 1 pF (photodiode capacitance).
- **ALC disabled** ($\overline{\text{IDC\_EN}}$ = 3.3 V).
- $R_L$ = 100 Ω differential, $T_A$ = 25 °C.

## Architecture (p1, p3, p20–p23)

The signal path runs IN → TIA stage → fixed-gain single-ended-to-differential ADC-driver stage → OUT± (p20).
- The TIA feedback resistor is switched between 1 kΩ and 10 kΩ by the GAIN pin.
- Each output has an internal 10 Ω series resistor.

Built-in functions:
- An integrated 100 mA input clamp.
- An **ambient-light-cancellation (ALC) loop**, which injects a DC current at IN.
- VOCM and VOD pins, which set the output common mode and the differential output offset.
- An EN pin that powers the part down and puts the outputs in high impedance, so outputs can be multiplexed.

The IN pin self-biases at **2.47 V**.

The part is optimized for the photodiode *sinking* photocurrent: cathode to IN, anode to a negative bias, so current flows out of IN. The ALC loop, the VOD pin and the fast-recovery clamp work only in this orientation.

## Transimpedance
| Setting | Into 100 Ω differential | Unloaded | Source |
|---|---|---|---|
| GAIN low or floating (default) | 1.75 / **2.0** / 2.25 kΩ (MIN/TYP/MAX) | 2.4 kΩ (TEXT) | p6, p21 |
| GAIN high | 17 / **20** / 22.5 kΩ | 24 kΩ | p7, p21 |

- **Gain with other loads:** $A_v = 2\,\text{k}\Omega\,(20\,\text{k}\Omega) \times 1.2 \times \dfrac{2R_{ADC}}{2R_{ADC} + 20\,\Omega}$ (Eq 2, p24).
- **Gain against temperature (CURVE, p16 Figs 6-33 and 6-34):**
  - 2 kΩ: about −100 ppm/°C.
  - 20 kΩ: about −600 ppm/°C; 20.3–20.6 kΩ at −40 °C falls to 18.3–18.6 kΩ at 125 °C.

- 2 kΩ: **250 nA_RMS**, with "f = 500 MHz" as the test condition.
- 20 kΩ: **49 nA_RMS**, "f = 250 MHz".

The 0.5 pF and 2 pF curves lie within ±0.4 pA/√Hz of the 1 pF curve below 200 MHz.

**Integrated over 10–200 MHz at $C_{PD}$ = 1 pF (DERIVED from the curves):**
- 2 kΩ: **122 nA_RMS**, an RMS-average density of 8.9 pA/√Hz.
- 20 kΩ: **34 nA_RMS**, or 2.45 pA/√Hz.

The same curves integrated over 1–490 MHz and 1–245 MHz give 202 nA and 39 nA. These are about 25 % below the EC figures; the datasheet does not state how the EC values were measured.

**Against temperature, 20 kΩ** (Fig 6-17, mean over 10–200 MHz): 1.85 pA/√Hz at −40 °C, 2.25 at 25 °C, 2.82 at 125 °C.

## Maximum DC input current

**ALC disabled (EC):**
- 2 kΩ: **600 µA MIN, 705 µA TYP** (p6).
- 20 kΩ: **60 µA MIN, 72 µA TYP** (p7).
- The criterion is "$Z_{21}$ < 3 dB degradation".
- The text gives "≈ 650 µA and 65 µA", for a photodiode sinking current (p21).

**Gain against DC input current, 2 kΩ at 25 °C** (CURVE, p16 Fig 6-31; positive current means current out of IN into the photodiode cathode):
- Flat from **−300 µA to +700 µA**.
- −1 dB at about +795 µA and −418 µA.
- −3 dB at about +1.0 mA and −550 µA.

**Same, 20 kΩ** (Fig 6-32): flat from −25 µA to +75 µA; −1 dB at about +87 µA and −38 µA.

**Limits and protection:**
- Absolute maximum continuous current into IN: **25 mA** (p5).
- The clamp handles input pulses up to 100 mA (TEXT, p21).

**Input resistance:** 60 / 100 / 120 Ω at 2 kΩ and 270 / 350 / 410 Ω at 20 kΩ (p6, p7).

## DC / ambient-light cancellation loop (ALC)

- **Control:** $\overline{\text{IDC\_EN}}$ low enables the loop (this is the pin-table default); high disables it (p3, p10).
- **Polarity:** works only with a photodiode that **sinks** current (p23).
- **Range:** **2 mA MIN, 3 mA TYP**, defined as an output-offset shift of less than ±10 mV relative to $I_{DC}$ = 10 µA (p9).
- **Range against temperature** (CURVE, p17 Fig 6-42): the 10 mV droop is reached at 2.18 mA at 125 °C, 2.82 mA at 25 °C and 3.34 mA at −40 °C. Beyond that the output collapses.
- **Loop high-pass corner:**
  - The text gives "100 kHz" (p23).
  - Fig 6-9 at $I_{DC}$ = 100 µA shows **52 kHz** at 2 kΩ and **374 kHz** at 20 kΩ, the latter with +0.86 dB of peaking near 2 MHz (CURVE).
- **Settling (TYP, p9):**

  | Step | 2 kΩ | 20 kΩ |
  |---|---|---|
  | Turn-on step (0 → 100 µA at 2 kΩ, 0 → 10 µA at 20 kΩ) | 18 µs | 2.5 µs |
  | Turn-off step (back to 0) | 35 µs | 13 µs |

- **Noise penalty:** the datasheet says the loop adds noise, namely the shot noise of the cancellation current (p9, p23). Figs 6-15 and 6-16 (CURVE and DERIVED, 10–200 MHz, RMS-average density):
  - 2 kΩ: 9.25 pA/√Hz at 0 µA, 9.85 at 100 µA, **11.3 at 1 mA**. Integrated: 128, 136 and 155 nA_RMS.
  - 20 kΩ: 2.87 pA/√Hz at 0 µA and **5.6 at 1 mA**. Integrated: 40 and 77 nA.
  - Below 10 MHz there is a large hump: 51 pA/√Hz at 200 kHz (2 kΩ, 1 mA).
  - The added noise at 1 mA (about 6.5 pA/√Hz, DERIVED) is smaller than the 17.9 pA/√Hz shot noise of the 1 mA photocurrent itself.

## Output

**Topology and drive:**
- The output is differential, with 10 Ω in series with each pin. It is designed to drive 100 Ω differential or an ADC directly (p1, p21, p24).
- **Output swing:**
  - The features list gives 1.5 Vpp.
  - VOH is 2.87 V MIN / 2.9 V TYP; VOL is 0.36 V TYP / 0.39 V MAX (p8).
  - With VOD = 0 only **0.75 Vpp** differential is usable. Setting VOD to about 0.75 V doubles this to about 1.5 Vpp (p22, p27, Figs 6-39 and 6-40).
- **Linear output current:** 24 / 26.6 / 32 mA into 25 Ω.
- **Short-circuit current:** 70 mA differential. The part cannot withstand a continuous short (p8).

**Output impedance:**
- 18 / 21 / 24 Ω differential when enabled; 3.3 kΩ in shutdown (p8).
- Against frequency: 23 Ω at 100 MHz, 36 Ω at 200 MHz, 133 Ω at 500 MHz (p12 Fig 6-10, CURVE).

**VOCM pin (p8):**
- Default 1.1 V (1.05–1.15 V); usable range 0.7–2.3 V.
- Bandwidth 285 MHz small-signal, 85 MHz large-signal.
- Noise 17.8 nV/√Hz with a 1 nF capacitor on the pin.

**VOD pin (p9):**
- Default output offset 510 mV (490–530 mV).
- Gain 1.01 V/V, bandwidth 45 MHz.
- Output offset with VOD = 0: 3.5 ± 12 mV at 2 kΩ and 5 ± 20 mV at 20 kΩ.

**Single-ended use:** allowed, but the signal halves and the common-mode noise adds (p22).

## Overload and clamp

**Clamps (p20, p21):**
- With a sinking photodiode, the fast-recovery clamp acts.
- With a sourcing photodiode, a soft clamp acts; its recovery is slower and depends on the overload.

**Pulse extension:** 4 ns TYP for a 10 mA, 10 ns pulse at both gains (p6, p7).

**Overload pulses (CURVE):**
- Sinking photodiode (Figs 6-21 and 6-22): 1 mA clips at about 1.45–1.48 V with about 1 ns of stretch.
- Sourcing photodiode (Figs 8-6 and 8-7):
  - 2 kΩ: 2.5 mA stretches the pulse by about 2.3 ns.
  - 20 kΩ: 1 mA stretches it by about 6 ns.

**Switching times (p10):**
- Gain change: high to low 90 ns; low to high 750 ns; 4 µs with ALC on.
- Enable: 125 ns (2 kΩ) and 850 ns (20 kΩ) with ALC off; 10 µs and 4 µs with ALC on.

## Supply, power, package

**Supply and power:**
- $V_{DD}$: 3.0 / 3.3 / 3.45 V; absolute maximum 3.65 V (p5).
- $I_Q$: 24 / 30 / 33.5 mA, giving about **99 mW** (DERIVED); 3.3 mA in shutdown (p9).
- PSRR: 54 dB MIN, 66 dB TYP (p9).
- VDD1 and VDD2 must come from the same supply with separate bypassing. Bipolar supplies are possible (p28).

**Ratings:**
- Temperature range: −40 to +125 °C.
- ESD: HBM ±1 kV, CDM ±250 V.
- $R_{θJA}$ = 56.3 °C/W (p5).

**VQFN-16 package (RGT0016C):**
- Body 3 × 3 mm, 0.5 mm pitch, 0.8–1.0 mm high.
- Exposed pad 1.68 ± 0.07 mm, to be connected to GND or the most negative supply (p3, p28, p37).
- Orderables LMH32401IRGTR and LMH32401IRGTT; MSL 1.

**Bare die (LMH32401YR):**
- 1.025 × 1.060 mm, 381 µm thick.
- The backside must be held at the most negative supply (p1, p4).

## Inconsistencies in the datasheet
- The EC integrated noise is about 25 % higher than the integral of the published density curves.
- The ALC corner is given as 100 kHz in the text but plotted at 52 kHz (2 kΩ) and 374 kHz (20 kΩ).
- Eq 1 on p22 is printed with "R_L × 20 Ω" where "+ 20 Ω" is meant.
- The input-bias-voltage drift is 1.1 mV/°C in the EC but about 0.73 mV/°C in Fig 6-36.
- The y-axis unit of Fig 6-18 should presumably be nV/√Hz.
- The thermal table says "12 pins" for a 16-pin package.
- The EC DC input range (705 µA at "3 dB") is tighter than the −3 dB point read from Fig 6-31 (about 1 mA).
