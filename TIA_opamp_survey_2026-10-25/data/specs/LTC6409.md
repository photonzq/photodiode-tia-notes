# LTC6409 — specification extract (fully differential amplifier)

**Source:** `datasheets/ltc6409_distributor_copy.pdf`, Linear Technology (now Analog Devices) LTC6409 data sheet, document code 6409fb, footer "LT 0517 REV B". Revision history: Rev A 12/10, Rev B 05/17. Production data sheet.

**Why it is here.** It is a fully differential amplifier, listed only as an alternative architecture: a balanced/differential TIA or a post-amplifier.

**Citation convention.** "pN" is the PDF page index and equals the printed page number. Graphs are cited by title and their graph code (e.g. "G18"). Value types: **TYP**; **MIN/MAX (25 °C)**; **(over temp)** marks rows with the full-temperature bullet; **CURVE** values were read from the plot's vector path after calibrating against the gridlines and were checked against renders; **DERIVED** values are computed here and are not printed. Electrical-characteristics conditions: $T_A = 25\,^\circ$C, $V^+ = 5$ V, $V^- = 0$ V, $V_{CM} = V_{OCM} = V_{ICM} = 1.25$ V, SHDN open.

## 1. Stability requirement and compensation

| Item | Value | Type | Source |
|---|---|---|---|
| Minimum stable configuration | Differential gain 1 V/V, i.e. $R_I = R_F$, feedback factor $\beta = R_I/(R_I+R_F) = 1/2$ ("designed to be stable in a differential signal gain of 1") | statement | p1, p16 |
| Recommended at gain 1 | $R_I = R_F = 150\ \Omega$ with $C_F = 1.3$ pF across each $R_F$ (keeps peaking below 1 dB and maximises $f_{-3\,\mathrm{dB}}$) | statement | p16 |
| GBW | 10 typ / 9.5 min GHz (25 °C), 8 GHz min over temperature. Measured with $R_I = 25\ \Omega$, $R_F = 10$ kΩ at $f_\mathrm{TEST} = 100$ MHz | TYP/MIN | p4 |
| Open-loop DC gain $A_{VOL}$ | 65 dB | TYP | p3 |
| Compensation | "intentionally compensated in such a way that its GBW is significantly larger than its $f_{-3\,\mathrm{dB}}$", i.e. not a single-pole roll-off | statement | p16 |
| Open-loop gain/phase plot | **not published**; no phase-margin figure anywhere | — | — |

**Feedback-capacitor guidance (p16).** $C_F$ in parallel with each $R_F$ "implements a pole-zero pair ... adds positive phase to the feedback loop gain ... boosts the phase margin". A larger $C_F$ reduces peaking and bandwidth. At $A_V = 5$, $C_F = 0.8$ pF gives the maximum $f_{-3\,\mathrm{dB}}$ and $C_F = 1.2$ pF gives no overshoot (p24 circuit).

**Layout warning relevant to a capacitive source (p17).** "It is highly recommended that any stray capacitance at the input pins, +IN and −IN, be kept to an absolute minimum ... especially true when the feedback resistor network uses resistor values greater than 500 Ω in circuits with $R_I = R_F$."

**Load capacitance.** Decouple each output with at least 10 Ω in series (p9, p17). G16 (p7), with no series resistor and $R_I = R_F = 150\ \Omega$, $C_F = 1.3$ pF, $C_L$ from each output to ground: peaking +0.4 dB (0 pF), +0.8 dB (0.5 pF), +6.4 dB (1 pF), +13.9 dB (1.5 pF), +11 dB (2 pF), all near 1.8–2.1 GHz (CURVE ±1 dB).

**Operation below the minimum** ($\beta > 1/2$) is not discussed. The datasheet has no TIA, photodiode or capacitive-source example. "Time Domain Reflectometry" is listed as an application on p1.

### Recommended feedback networks (G15 table, p7) and measured −3 dB bandwidth

$V_S = 5$ V, $R_{LOAD} = 400\ \Omega$. Bandwidths are CURVE readings, ±5 %.

| $A_V$ | $R_I$ (Ω) | $R_F$ (Ω) | $C_F$ (pF) | −3 dB |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 150 | 150 | 1.3 | 2.0 GHz (spec 2 GHz TYP, p4; peak +0.3 dB at 1 GHz) |
| 2 | 100 | 200 | 1 | 1.5 GHz |
| 5 | 50 | 250 | 0.8 | 1.04 GHz |
| 10 | 50 | 500 | 0.4 | 0.70 GHz |
| 20 | 25 | 500 | 0.4 | 0.58 GHz |
| 100 | 25 | 2.5k | 0 | 114 MHz |
| 400 | 25 | 10k | 0 | 23 MHz |

The 0.1 dB flatness is 600 MHz TYP and the full-power bandwidth is 550 MHz TYP at $V_{OUTDIFF}$ = 2 V p-p (p4).

**Approximate open-loop magnitude (DERIVED).** Above ≈ 200 MHz the $A_V$ = 400 and $A_V$ = 100 curves of G15 merge. Where the loop gain is much less than 1, the $A_V$ = 400 curve approximates $\lvert A_{OL}\rvert$, to within ≈ 0.5 dB above 300 MHz; at 100–200 MHz it may read up to ≈ 2 dB low. The values are 40.3 dB (100 MHz), 34.2 (200 MHz), 30.3 (300 MHz), 25.3 (500 MHz), 21.8 (700 MHz), 16.7 (1 GHz), 9.8 (1.5 GHz), 5.2 (2 GHz), 1.9 (2.5 GHz) and −2.6 dB (3 GHz). This gives a 0 dB crossing near 2.7 GHz, with a slope of ≈ 25 dB/decade from 0.2 to 1 GHz and ≈ 40 dB/decade from 1 to 3 GHz. No phase data are available.

IMD3 (two tones 200 kHz apart, 2 V p-p envelope): −110 dBc (25 MHz), −98 (100 MHz), −88 dBc (140 MHz) TYP (p4). Best distortion is obtained with 200 Ω – 1 kΩ load per output (p13). Distortion depends strongly on the output common-mode level (G20, G23, G26).

## 6. Package and pinout

| Package | Body | Pitch | Exposed pad | θJA / θJC (°C/W) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| UDB 10-lead plastic QFN (LTC DWG 05-08-1848 Rev A; not JEDEC) | 3.00 × 2.00 × 0.75 mm | 0.50 mm | pin 11, 0.90 × 0.25 mm strip | 138 / 5.2 |

Tie the exposed pad to V−. With split supplies, "DO NOT tie the pad to ground" (p8). $T_{J,\max}$ is 150 °C. Pins: 1 −OUT, 2 +IN, 3 SHDN, 4 V+, 5 VOCM, 6 −IN, 7 +OUT, 8 V−, 9 V+, 10 V−, 11 EP (V−). Grades: C (0–70 °C), I (−40 to 85 °C), H (−40 to 125 °C); marking LFPF (p2, p8, p22).

**Absolute maximum and protection (p2, p4, p9).** Total supply 5.5 V. Input current ±10 mA. There are steering diodes to both supplies, and back-to-back diode pairs between +IN and −IN; if the differential input exceeds 1.4 V, limit the current to < 10 mA. Output short-circuit duration is indefinite (a heat sink may be needed). Storage −65 to 150 °C.

**Layout and bypassing (p17).** Use 0.1 µF ∥ 1000 pF from each V+ to its nearest V−, plus capacitors to ground with split supplies. Bypass VOCM with ≥ 0.01 µF. Keep output loads balanced. 0.1 % resistors give about 54 dB worst-case CMRR (p11).

## 7. Caveats

1. No open-loop gain/phase data are published; the open-loop magnitude above is an approximation derived from closed-loop curves.
2. The noise traces in G18 are idealised polylines. The plotted $i_n$ plateau (8.4) is slightly below the table value (8.8 pA/√Hz).
3. The block-diagram $V_{OCM}$ divider (200 k / 50 k) would give 1.0 V, but the self-bias is specified as 1.25 V. Not explained.
4. Input bias current is large and flows out of the pins: −70 µA typ, up to −160 µA.
