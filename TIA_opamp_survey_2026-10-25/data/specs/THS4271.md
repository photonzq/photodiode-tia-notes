## Minimum stable gain

- **value**: 1
- **unit**: V/V
- **conditions**: Datasheet title 'Unity gain stable'; 1.4 GHz small-signal bandwidth at G = +1, RL = 150 ohm (pdf p.4).
- **what_datasheet_says_below_or_at_min**:
  - Unity gain in the SOIC (D) package 'may result in the device becoming unstable'; SOIC is suitable for gains >= +2 V/V or -1 V/V; TI recommends the leadless MSOP (DRB) or MSOP PowerPAD (DGN) for maximum performance (pdf p.31, Design Tools).
  - Fig 92 (pdf p.31): unity-gain follower, VIN = 100 mVpp, +-5 V, 150-ohm load: SOIC with Rf = 0 ohm peaks by about +17.5 dB near 650 MHz; SOIC Rf = 100 ohm about +10 dB near 500 MHz; SOIC Rf = 200 ohm about +8.5 dB near 380 MHz; leadless MSOP and MSOP with Rf = 0 ohm about +2.5 dB near 600 MHz (CURVE, +-1 dB).
  - The general gain-configurable EVM 'can be configured in a gain of +1; however, it is strongly not recommended ... may cause the part to become unstable'; stability can be controlled with a large feedback resistor at the cost of performance (pdf p.31). Fig 93: on that EVM peaking of about +16 dB (Rf = 0) to about +5.5 dB (Rf = 150 ohm) (CURVE, +-1 dB).
  - Peaking is attributed to lead inductance in the feedback path; recommended maximum feedback-path inductance 4 nH, with a trace-inductance formula (Eq. 8) (pdf p.32).
  - 'nominally compensated to operate with a 2-pF parasitic load'; parasitic capacitive loads > 2 pF can begin to degrade performance; loads < 4 pF may not need an isolation resistor (pdf p.27-28).
  - No guidance is given on capacitive (TIA) feedback or noise-gain shaping; the datasheet contains no transimpedance example.

## Pinout notes

THS4271: 1 NC, 2 IN-, 3 IN+, 4 VS-, 5 NC, 6 VOUT, 7 VS+, 8 NC (all packages D, DRB, DGN, DGK). THS4275: 1 REF, 2 IN-, 3 IN+, 4 VS-, 5 NC, 6 VOUT, 7 VS+, 8 PD-bar (pdf p.3). Standard single op-amp pinout, so the feedback resistor can sit between pins 2 and 6.

## Application guidance

- Keep the die below 60C to avoid low-level oscillation (see other_notes); THS4211/THS4215 are the recommended replacements (pdf p.1, p.19).
- Minimise parasitic capacitance on all signal I/O pins; open ground and power planes around the I/O pins (pdf p.28).
- Decouple with 0.1 uF placed < 0.25 inch from the supply pins plus 2.2-6.8 uF bulk; 6.8-22 uF bulk, 0.1 uF and 1000 pF recommended per supply (pdf p.23, p.28).
- Place the feedback resistor directly under the package on the opposite side between output and inverting input when double-sided mounting is possible (pdf p.28).
- Socketing not recommended (pdf p.29).
- Capacitive loads: isolation resistor R(ISO) recommended; Fig 24 (pdf p.12): 25 ohm for 10 pF, 15 ohm for 50 pF, 10 ohm for 100 pF (RL = 499, +-5 V). Fig 88 (pdf p.27) pairs 15 ohm with 100 pF and 10 ohm with 50 pF (inconsistent with Fig 24; rev F says Figure 24 notation was corrected).
- Load of 150 ohm to 1 kohm gives best distortion; terminating an ADC input in 50 ohm can be detrimental (pdf p.26).

## Absolute maximum ratings and input protection

VS 16.5 V; VI +-VS; IO 100 mA; TJ 150C max, 125C for continuous long-term reliability, 60C max to prevent oscillation; Tstg -65 to 150C; ESD HBM 3000 V, CDM 1500 V, MM 1000 V (pdf p.2). No statement on input-protection diodes or maximum differential input voltage. No thermal shutdown (pdf p.30).

## Other notes, inconsistencies and uncertainties

- MAJOR CAVEAT: 'The THS4271 and THS4275 may have low-level oscillation when the die temperature ... exceeds +60C'; caused by the internal bias circuit, not mitigated by external configuration, occurs randomly with process variation and cannot be screened (pdf p.1, p.19). Table 1 (pdf p.19) gives the maximum ambient temperature for TJ < 60C at IQ = 27 mA and RL = 499 ohm: SOIC 28.8-33.7C, VSON 45.3-47.6C, PowerPAD MSOP 41.3-44.2C, MSOP (DGK) -23.2 to -10.2C. At +-5 V the quiescent dissipation alone is about 0.22-0.27 W.
- THS4275 power-down: PD defaults to positive supply (amplifier on) when not driven; thresholds relative to REF: enable >= REF + 1.8 V, disable <= REF + 1 V when REF <= midrail; enable >= REF - 1 V, disable <= REF - 1.7 V when REF > midrail (Tables 2 and 3, pdf p.22-23). PD quiescent current 875 uA TYP (REF = 0, PD = REF + 1 V, +-5 V; MAX 1000/1100/1200 uA) or 650 uA TYP (PD = REF - 1.7 V, REF = VS+); 5 V: 650 uA TYP both. Turn-on 4 us, turn-off 3 us (50% of supply current). Power-down output impedance 200 kohm (+-5 V) / 100 kohm (5 V); PD input impedance 4 Gohm / 6 Gohm at 1 MHz (pdf p.5, p.7). Power-down is not a high-impedance output mode (pdf p.22).
- Differential gain/phase 0.007% / 0.004 deg (G = 2, RL = 150) (pdf p.4).
- SPICE model available; does not model distortion or package differences (pdf p.35).
- Other qualified version: THS4271-EP (pdf p.38).
