## Minimum gain and operation below it

- Stable in noise gain ≥ 7 V/V (16.9 dB); at NG = 7: 790 MHz SSBW, ~50° phase margin (p16 Sec. 7.3.3). The second $A_{OL}$ pole lies above the 7 V/V crossover but before 0 dB, so it is not unity-gain stable (p15).
- "Designed for use in applications with lower gains by applying noise-gain shaping techniques" (p1). In a TIA the high-frequency noise gain is $1 + C_{TOT}/C_F$-type ratio > 1, so a unity-gain-stable amplifier is not required (p16).
- Sec. 8.2.2 (p22): $G = 2$ V/V example with 10 Ω + 10 pF between IN+ and IN−, $R_G = 301\,\Omega$, $R_F = 301\,\Omega \parallel 3.3$ pF → ~51° PM, flat beyond 100 MHz. "A 45° phase margin is generally acceptable, but anything less than 40° is not recommended"; 1–2 pF of inverting-node parasitic requires retuning.

## Noise

**Voltage noise** (Fig. 6-19, p10, not labelled simulation; plotted 100 Hz–1 MHz only): 25.5 nV/√Hz @102 Hz, 8.40 @1 kHz, 3.32 @10 kHz, 2.97 @15 kHz, 2.57 @30 kHz, 2.25 @100 kHz, 2.15 @300 kHz, 2.12 @0.98 MHz (CURVE ±2 %). EC: 2.2 nV/√Hz for $f \ge 150$ kHz, 1/f corner 15 kHz (p5). No $e_n$ data above 1 MHz; Fig. 8-8 output noise of the $G=7$ circuit (16.5 nV/√Hz, flat to ~100 MHz) is consistent with a flat $e_n$.

**Current noise** (Fig. 6-20, p10, labelled **"Common-mode noise"** and **"Simulation"**; 10 kHz–100 MHz):

- EC spot values (p5): 3 fA/√Hz @10 kHz, 145 fA/√Hz @1 MHz (TYP).
- Shape (DERIVED from the curve): ∝ $f^{0.9\text{–}1}$ from 10 kHz to ~2 MHz; shoulder (slope ~0.4–0.6) from ~3 to ~30 MHz; slope ~0.85 between 50 and 100 MHz. No data above 100 MHz.
- The datasheet gives no physical explanation of the rise and no definition of "common-mode noise"; p21 states the TINA-TI model "closely models the voltage and current noise". It does not say whether this $i_n$ already contains the $e_n \cdot 2\pi f C_{in}$ term.
- DERIVED check: $i_n = e_n \cdot 2\pi f\, C$ with $e_n = 2.2$ nV/√Hz would need $C$ = 10.3 pF at 1 MHz, 4.3 pF at 10 MHz, 1.85 pF at 100 MHz — not a fixed capacitance, and well above the 2.4 pF input capacitance below ~20 MHz.

## Input capacitance and input range

- EC (p5): common-mode input impedance 500 GΩ ∥ 1.9 pF; differential input impedance 500 GΩ ∥ 0.5 pF (TYP). p1: "2.4pF total input capacitance"; p16: "2.4 pF combined common-mode and differential". Fig. 8-3 (p20) draws both from the inverting node to ground: $C_{TOT} = C_D + C_{DIFF} + C_{CM}$. TINA-TI model contains both (p21).
- Input common-mode range (p5–p6): most positive input $V_{S+} - 3.6$ V min / $V_{S+} - 3.2$ V typ; most negative $V_{S-}$ typ / $V_{S-} + 0.25$ V max (ΔV_OS ≤ ±1 mV at the limits). At ±5 V: −5 V (−4.75 V guaranteed) to +1.4 V guaranteed (+1.8 V typ) (DERIVED). Ground is outside the range below ±4 V supplies (p17).
- CMRR 90 dB typ / 73 min (70 over temp); PSRR+ 95 typ / 75 min (70 over temp); PSRR− 94 typ / 80 min (74 over temp) (p5–p6). Fig. 6-12 (SIM, p8): CMRR 84 dB @1 MHz, 64 @10 MHz, 44 @100 MHz; PSRR+ 76/55 dB, PSRR− 74/56 dB at 1/10 MHz.

## TIA content in the datasheet

1. **Front-page plot "Photodiode Capacitance vs 3dB Bandwidth"** (p1): $R_F$ = 20k/50k/100k/500k, $C_D$ = 0.5–20 pF. $R_F$ = 20 kΩ: 86.1 MHz @0.5 pF, 79.5 @1 pF, 69.9 @2 pF, 53.9 @5 pF, 41.6 @10 pF; 50 kΩ: 54.4/50.3/44.2/34.1/26.3 MHz; 100 kΩ: 38.5/35.6/31.3/24.1/18.6 MHz; 500 kΩ: 17.2/15.9/14.0/10.8/8.3 MHz. Not labelled measured or simulated; DERIVED check: all values equal Eq. 1 with GBWP = 2.7 GHz and $C_{TOT} = C_D + 2.4$ pF (no PCB parasitic) within 0.2 % → a calculation.
2. **Eq. 1** (p19, Butterworth): $f_{-3dB} = \sqrt{\mathrm{GBWP}/(2\pi R_F C_{TOT})}$; $C_{TOT}$ = photodiode + amplifier CM and differential capacitance + PCB parasitic; $R_F C_{TOT}$ zero in the noise gain must be compensated by $C_F$ (p16, p19).
3. **100 kΩ design example** (p20–p21, Fig. 8-3/8-4/8-5, Tables 8-1/8-2): $C_D$ = 5 pF, $C_{TOT}$ = 7.4 pF, target 24 MHz → calculator GBWP 2678.14 MHz, $C_F$ = 0.092 pF (Q = 0.707, ~65° PM, 4.3 % overshoot), built as tee $C_1 = C_2 = 2.2$ pF, $C_T$ = 47 pF. TINA-TI simulated $f_{-3dB}$ = 24.6 MHz, ~0.1 dB peaking. Simulated noise: 41 nV/√Hz flat-band output (0.41 pA/√Hz input-referred; $R_F$ thermal 40.7 nV/√Hz), peak ~245 nV/√Hz (~2.45 pA/√Hz) at ~22 MHz (CURVE).
4. Output-swing guidance (p19): photocurrent pulls the output below $V_{REF}$; $V_{REF}$ may be set closer to $V_{S+}$ within the input CM range. Front-page figure: OPA818 TIA ($R_F$ = 100 kΩ) → THS4541 → ADC.
5. Layout (p23): if $C_F$ < 0.15 pF, use two series resistors of $R_F/2$. (p24–p25): photodiode on the same board side; $R_{ISO}$ = 10–20 Ω next to IN−; close the $R_F$/$C_F$/$R_{ISO}$ loop at the photodiode pins; Eq. 4 noise gain $1 + Z_F/Z_{IN}$.
6. p16: "Transimpedance gains of 50 kΩ or greater benefit from the low-current-noise JFET input."

DERIVED for this survey ($R_F$ = 1.5–2 kΩ, $C_D$ = 1 pF, $C_{TOT}$ = 3.4 pF without PCB parasitic): Eq. 1 gives 290 MHz (1.5 kΩ) and 251 MHz (2 kΩ); the high-frequency noise gain then sits near the 7 V/V minimum, so the PM data above apply.

## Inconsistencies / uncertainties

- PD polarity: pin table (p3) "low = enable, high = disable" vs EC thresholds (p6) and Fig. 8-9 "Connect PD to VS+ to enable".
- Supply: Sec. 8.3 "6 V to 12 V" vs 13 V max (p4, p6) and ±6.5 V (p2). $T_J$: "do not exceed 105 °C" (p24) vs abs max 125 °C (p4).
- $I_B$: 4 pA typ in EC and histogram, but ~25–30 pA on the 25 °C curve of Fig. 6-30 (conditions not stated).
- $i_n$: 3 fA/√Hz (EC) vs ~2.5 fA/√Hz on the simulated curve at 10 kHz; the curve is a simulation of "common-mode noise" with no further definition.
- Eq. 3 (p21) has $C_{EQ}$ on the left where $C_T$ is meant, and the text says "set $C_{EQ} = C_{TOT}$" where $C_{EQ} = C_F$ is meant (47 pF follows from 0.092 pF).
- Sec. 8.2.2 quotes "64 nV/√Hz" and "166 nV/√Hz" at 100 MHz; in Fig. 8-8 these match the integrated noise in µVrms, not the density.
- SSBW at NG = 7: 765 MHz (EC) vs 790 MHz (text). $A_{OL}$: 92 dB (EC) vs ~94 dB (SIM plot).
