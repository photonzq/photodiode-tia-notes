## Minimum stable gain

- **value**: 1
- **unit**: V/V
- **conditions**: Device Comparison Table (pdf p.3); 'stable for unity gain applications' (pdf p.19). Phase margin about 57 deg as a unity-gain buffer (pdf p.16); simulated phase-margin spread < 5 deg within one standard deviation of process (pdf p.16, SIM).
- **what_datasheet_says_below_or_at_min**: Not applicable (unity-gain stable). For TIA use: RF with the total input capacitance CIN forms a zero in the noise gain that causes instability if left unchecked; a feedback capacitor CF adds the compensating pole; design equations are deferred to TI application report 'Transimpedance Considerations for High-Speed Amplifiers' and an Excel calculator (pdf p.20). Unity-gain stability permits feedback clamping (diode across RF) that would not work with a decompensated amplifier (pdf p.19, Fig 9-1). If the required CF is below 0.15 pF, use two series resistors of RF/2 each to reduce the resistor parasitic capacitance (pdf p.24).

## Pinout notes

1 FB (feedback pin, internally connected to the output), 2 NC (must be left floating; isolates FB from IN-), 3 IN-, 4 IN+, 5 VS-, 6 OUT, 7 VS+, 8 PD-bar (logic low = power off, high = normal; tie to VS+ to enable), thermal pad to VS- (pdf p.3, p.15, p.24). RF connects between FB (pin 1) and IN- (pin 3) on the same side of the package, so the feedback loop does not go around the package; the NC pin increases FB-to-IN- spacing and reduces their capacitive coupling (Fig 8-4, pdf p.15). PD thresholds are referenced to VS-: disable below 1 V TYP (0.65 MIN), enable above 1.5 V TYP (1.8 MAX), with hysteresis (pdf p.6, p.18). In power-down the output is high impedance, but if the differential input exceeds a diode drop the input protection diodes create a low-impedance path from IN+ to the output (pdf p.18).

## Application guidance

- TIA bandwidth depends on total CIN (photodiode + amplifier CCM + CDIFF + PCB stray), GBWP and RF; CF sets the compensating pole (pdf p.20).
- If the required CF is below 0.15 pF, use two series resistors of RF/2 each to cut the parasitic capacitance of the feedback resistor (pdf p.24).
- Cut the power and ground planes under the signal input and output pins; keep planes unbroken elsewhere (pdf p.24).
- Bypass within 0.25 inch with 100-pF to 0.1-uF C0G/NP0 capacitors rated at least 3x the supply, plus 2.2-6.8 uF farther away (pdf p.24); Fig 10-1 uses 0.1 uF + 6.8 uF per supply (pdf p.23).
- Place RF and the series output resistor next to the output and inverting-input pins; use low-reactance surface-mount resistors; never wirewound (pdf p.24).
- In single-supply operation, bias the input common mode and the output reference at midsupply from a low-impedance source (pdf p.18).
- Gain-stage example values: RF = 453 ohm, RG = 75 ohm, RT = 49.9 ohm, 200-ohm load (Fig 10-1, pdf p.23).

## Absolute maximum ratings and input protection

VS 5.5 V; input voltage (VS-) - 0.5 V to (VS+) + 0.5 V; differential input voltage 1 V; output voltage (VS-) - 0.5 V to (VS+) + 0.5 V; continuous input current +-10 mA; continuous output current +-100 mA (electromigration); TJ 150C; TA -40 to 125C; Tstg -65 to 150C; ESD HBM +-1500 V, CDM +-1500 V (pdf p.4). Inputs have two antiparallel diodes between IN+ and IN- plus ESD diodes to both supplies on all pins (Fig 8-3, pdf p.15).

## Other notes, inconsistencies and uncertainties

- Inconsistency: section 8.3.4 states a slew rate of 2750 V/us (pdf p.17), versus 350 V/us in the table (pdf p.5) and 365/346 V/us in Fig 7-18 (pdf p.9).
- Inconsistency: AOL table typical 76 dB (pdf p.5) versus about 79-80 dB on Figs 7-7 and 8-5 at 25C.
- Section 8.1 text states 1.1 GHz GBWP and 0.9 nV/rtHz broadband noise; the table gives GBWP 1.08 GHz (pdf p.5, p.14).
- Table symbol for current noise is printed as 'ei' (pdf p.5).
- The front-page TIA chart does not state CF, supply or the noise-integration details beyond 'calculated'.
