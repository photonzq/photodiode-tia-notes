## Minimum stable gain

- **value**: 4
- **unit**: V/V
- **conditions**: 'stable for gains >= +4' (pdf p.1); 'gain of +4V/V stable op amp' (pdf p.19); gain peaking 7.5 dB TYP at G = +4 (pdf p.3).
- **what_datasheet_says_below_or_at_min**: Inconsistency: pdf p.21 states 'internally compensated to be unity-gain stable' in the same paragraph that gives 60 deg phase margin at a gain of +6, and then recommends the OPA698 if a unity-gain-stable amplifier is needed. Below the minimum gain: 'Low-gain compensation for improved SFDR' (pdf p.16-17, not read in detail) shapes the noise gain with a capacitor from the inverting input to ground and a feedback capacitor (front-page example: G = -2 with RG = 374 ohm, RF = 750 ohm, CS = 18 pF, CF = 4 pF, pdf p.1). For a photodiode TIA with large RF, the inverting-input capacitance causes peaking or oscillation; add a small capacitor in parallel with RF, which sets the bandwidth through the RF-CF pole, or use a T network (pdf p.21).

## output_limiting

- **mechanism**: Two buffered limiter inputs VH (pin 8) and VL (pin 5); when the output tries to exceed them the corresponding limiter buffer takes control of the output (output-side limiting, so accuracy does not change with gain and the internal signal path does not saturate) (pdf p.19).
- **limit_offset_accuracy**: +-10 mV TYP, +-30 mV MAX (25C), +-35 / +-40 mV over temperature (+-5 V); +-15 mV TYP, +-30 MAX (+5 V)
- **linear_to_within**: 30 mV of VH or VL (pdf p.19) [front page says 20 mV, pdf p.1]; linearity guardband 30 mV TYP (5 MHz, 2 Vpp, SFDR -3 dB criterion)
- **limit_range**: VL >= -VS + 0.7 V and VH <= +VS - 0.7 V; VH - VL >= 400 mV (pdf p.19); maximum limit voltage +-4.3 V (+-5 V); output voltage limited range +-3.8 V TYP (+-5 V)
- **default_limits_pins_open**: +-3.5 V TYP, +-3.3 V MIN (25C) at +-5 V; VCM +-1.1 V TYP (+-0.9 MIN) at +5 V
- **limiter_pin_bias_current**: 50 uA TYP magnitude (40 MIN / 60 MAX at 25C), IVH positive (out of pin 8) and IVL negative (into pin 5) at +-5 V; -15 uA at +5 V; limiter input impedance 3.4 Mohm || 1 pF
- **limiter_ac**: limiter small-signal bandwidth 600 MHz (+-5 V) / 450 MHz (+5 V); limiter slew rate 125 / 100 V/us; limiter feedthrough -60 dB at 5 MHz
- **op_amp_bias_shift**: input bias current shifts by 3 uA (+-5 V) / 5 uA (+5 V) between linear and limited operation
- **setting_guidance**: Resistor dividers are fine when limits are more than 2.1 V from the supplies (run >= 1 mA, e.g. 2.5 mA, through the divider and include the 50-uA limiter currents); within 2.1 V of the supplies use low-impedance buffers; bypass pins 5 and 8 (pdf p.20). Distortion is barely affected until the limits are within about +-1.1 V of a +-1 V output (Fig 13, pdf p.20).

## Application guidance

- Capacitive loads >= 2 pF need a series output resistor (recommended RS vs CL curve, pdf p.10; Fig 14, pdf p.20-21).
- Resistors > 1.5 kohm with about 0.2 pF parasitic add a pole/zero below 500 MHz; 750-ohm RF is the recommended starting point (pdf p.23).
- Do not use sockets (pdf p.23).

## Absolute maximum ratings and input protection

Supply +-6.5 V; input voltage +-VS; differential input +-VS; limiter voltage range +-(VS - 0.7 V); TJ 150C; storage -65 to 125C; ESD HBM 2000 V, MM 200 V, CDM 1000 V (pdf p.2). ESD diodes from every pin to both supplies, typically 30 mA continuous; add series resistors if driven from higher-voltage sources (pdf p.23).

## Other notes, inconsistencies and uncertainties

- Inconsistency: 'unity-gain stable' (pdf p.21) versus 'stable for gains >= +4' (pdf p.1, p.19).
- Inconsistency: linear to within 20 mV of the limits (pdf p.1) versus 30 mV (pdf p.19).
- Inconsistency: maximum total supply 13 V in text (pdf p.23) versus +-6 V / 12 V in the tables (pdf p.3, p.5).
