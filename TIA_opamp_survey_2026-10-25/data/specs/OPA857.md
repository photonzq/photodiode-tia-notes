# OPA857 — integrated transimpedance amplifier (Texas Instruments)

Datasheet: `datasheets/opa857.pdf`, SBOS630D (December 2013, revised August 2016), Production Data; package addendum dated 8 January 2026, with the orderables listed as Active.
Citations: pX is the PDF page index. For p1–p26 the printed page number is the same as the PDF index.
Value types:
- **TYP / MIN / MAX**: Electrical Characteristics (EC) values, with the datasheet's test level. Level A is 100 % tested at 25 °C, level B is set by characterization and simulation, and level C is a typical value given for information only.
- **CURVE**: a value read from a 300-dpi clip of a typical-characteristic plot.
- **DERIVED**: a value computed from datasheet numbers.
- **TEXT**: a statement taken from the datasheet's prose.

EC conditions unless noted (p6): $T_A$ = 25 °C, $V_S$ = 3.3 V, $C_{source}$ = 1.5 pF, $V_{OUT}$ = 0.5 Vpp differential, $R_L$ = 500 Ω differential.

## Architecture
- Single-ended current input with a pseudo-differential output (p1, p14):
  - OUT carries the signal.
  - OUTN carries the reference, set to 5/9 $V_S$ (1.83 V).
  - Each output has an internal 25 Ω series resistor.
- The core has a class-A output stage (p15).
- Two internal feedback resistors set the gain. CTRL selects between them (p17 Table 1), and CTRL also recompensates the core's open-loop gain (p21).
- An internal clamp and ESD diodes protect IN (p16).
- The photodiode must source current into IN, with its cathode tied to a positive bias. The output can only swing down from the reference (p18).
- **There is no DC or ambient-light cancellation on chip.**

## Input-referred current noise
- Integrated noise (p6, TYP C), measured with a 135 MHz brickwall filter at $C_s$ = 1.5 pF:
  - 25 nA_RMS at 5 k.
  - 15 nA_RMS at 20 k.
  - These correspond to mean densities of 2.15 and 1.29 pA/√Hz (DERIVED).
- Noise density, 5 k (p12 Fig 26, CURVE, ±5 %):
  - No added capacitance: 2.2 pA/√Hz at 10–20 MHz, 1.5 at 100 MHz, 0.8 at 200 MHz.
  - 1.5 pF: 2.3 pA/√Hz from 10 to 50 MHz, 2.1 at 100 MHz, 1.1 at 200 MHz.
  - 4.7 pF: 3.7 pA/√Hz at 50 MHz.
  - Mean over 10–100 MHz: 2.32 pA/√Hz at 1.5 pF and 3.30 at 4.7 pF.
- Noise density, 20 k (p12 Fig 27, CURVE):
  - No added capacitance: 1.1 pA/√Hz.
  - 1.5 pF: 1.3 pA/√Hz at 10 MHz, rising to 1.49 at 50 MHz, then 1.21 at 100 MHz.
  - Mean over 10–100 MHz: 1.41 pA/√Hz at 1.5 pF.
- On both plots the density falls above about 100 MHz. This probably means the output noise is referred through the low-frequency gain; that is an inference, not something the datasheet states.
- RMS noise against source capacitance (p9 Figs 9–10, CURVE):
  - 20 k: 14.0 nA at 1 pF, 16.7 at 2 pF, 24.2 at 5 pF.
  - 5 k: 24.5 nA at 1 pF, 28.0 at 2 pF, 40.3 at 5 pF.
- RMS noise against temperature (p9 Fig 7, 20 k): 13.7 nA at −40 °C, 15.5 at 25 °C, 16.8 at 95 °C.

## Maximum input current, DC handling
- Linear range: 60 µA at 20 k and 240 µA at 5 k (TEXT, p15). This follows from the 1.2 V output range, from 1.83 V down to 0.6 V.
- The input is unipolar: current flows into IN only.
- With the external 6.8 kΩ added at 5 k (2.88 kΩ), the linear range is about 0.42 mA (DERIVED).
- Absolute maximum input current at IN: 10 mA (p5). The p16 text says the ESD diodes "typically support 30-mA continuous", which conflicts with this.
- **No DC or ambient cancellation.** A DC photocurrent of 0.1–10 mA is out of range without an external DC-removal circuit.

## Output
- Output swing at OUT: 0.6 V MIN to 1.9 V MAX at 25 °C (A); 1.9 V MAX over −40 to 85 °C (B) (p6).
- OUTN reference: 1.78 / 1.83 / 1.88 V (A, p6).
- Linear output current into 50 Ω differential: +5 mA source, −20 mA sink (C, p6).
- Output current against temperature (p10 Fig 17, CURVE): sink 19.8 mA and source 4.1 mA at 25 °C.
- Output impedance: 50 Ω differential at 1 MHz (C, p6).
- A 100 Ω load cuts bandwidth to 68 MHz at 20 k and 89 MHz at 5 k (Figs 1 and 3). **50 Ω drive is not practical.**
- Output offset (p6): ±1 mV TYP and ±5 mV MAX at 25 °C (A); ±6 mV MAX over temperature (B); drift ±15 µV/°C MAX (C).

## Overload and clamp
- Overdrive recovery: 25 ns (TYP B) at 2× overload with CTRL = 1, settling to 1 % (p6). The features list on p1 says less than 25 ns.
- The clamp holds OUT at about 0.60 V (p10 Figs 13–14, CURVE).
- The output returns to linear operation within a few ns of the ideal output re-entering the range.

## Supply, power, package
- Supply: 2.7 / 3.3 / 3.6 V (ROC), absolute maximum 3.8 V (p5).
- Quiescent current (p7): 20.5 / 23.4 / 26.3 mA (A); 20.0–26.8 mA over temperature (B).
- Power: about 77 mW (DERIVED).
- PSRR (p7): 70 dB MIN and 80 dB TYP at DC (A); 15 dB MIN and 18 dB TYP at 10 MHz (B).
- Input pin capacitance: 2 pF (C, p6). The design target is 1.5 pF total, which includes 0.5–0.7 pF of photodiode capacitance (p14).
- Package: VQFN-16 (RGT0016A), 3 × 3 mm body, 0.5 mm pitch, 1 mm maximum height (p31).
  - Exposed pad: 1.45 ± 0.1 mm square. It must be soldered; the pin table does not state what it connects to (p4, p31).
  - $R_{θJA}$ = 67.1 °C/W (p5).
- Orderables: OPA857IRGTR and OPA857IRGTT (p27).

## Inconsistencies in the datasheet
- The 5 k bandwidth is 125 MHz on p6 but 130 MHz on p21.
- The reference is 1.83 V on p6 and p15 but 1.85 V on p18.
- Table 4 gives the load as "500 kΩ"; this is a typo for 500 Ω.
- The ESD diodes are said to carry 30 mA (p16), but the absolute maximum input current is 10 mA.
- The axes of Figs 35–36 are labelled mV but should be V.
- Figs 2, 4 and 17 are plotted up to 105–125 °C, beyond the 85 °C rating.
