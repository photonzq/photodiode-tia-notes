## Input type

bipolar ('very high-speed complementary bipolar process', PDF p20)

## Compensation

decompensated voltage feedback: optimized for a flat response at G = +20, stable down to a noise gain of +12 V/V (PDF p1). 'New external compensation techniques allow the OPA847 to be used at any inverting gain' (PDF p1).

## Minimum stable gain

- **value**: 12
- **unit**: V/V (noise gain)
- **conditions**: noninverting. Inverting with a matched 50 ohm source (noise gain 1 + RF/(2RG)): minimum inverting gain -22 V/V (PDF p16).
- **datasheet_statements**:
  - Features: 'STABLE FOR GAINS >= 12' (PDF p1); 'optimized for flat response at a gain of +20 ... stable down to +12V/V' (PDF p1).
  - Table: closed-loop bandwidth at G = +12 is 600 MHz typ with peaking 4.5 dB typ, 7 dB max at 25 C (PDF p3).
  - 'At an NG of 12, R1 is removed and only RF and RG are present in Figure 8. The peaking is typically 4.5dB' (PDF p14); noise gain below +12 gives 'increased peaking and possible sustained oscillations' (PDF p14).
  - Inverting with matched source: minimum inverting gain -22 and equivalent GBP > 7.8 GHz (PDF p16).
  - 'Unity-gain stability in the op amp is not required for application as a transimpedance amplifier' (PDF p11).
- **below_minimum_gain_methods**:
  - LOW-GAIN COMPENSATION FOR IMPROVED SFDR (PDF pp. 12-13): 'a new external compensation technique can be used to retain the full slew rate and noise benefits of the OPA847, while giving increased loop gain'. NG1 = 1 + RF/RG sets the low-frequency noise gain (resistor ratio); NG2 = 1 + CS/CF sets the high-frequency noise gain (capacitor ratio) and must exceed the minimum stable gain; noise-gain pole 1/(RF CF). Eq. 4: ZO = GBP/NG1^2 [(1 - NG1/NG2) - sqrt(1 - 2 NG1/NG2)]; ZO = 1/(2 pi RF (CF + CS)); zero at NG1 ZO, pole at NG2 ZO. Eq. 5: CF = 1/(2 pi RF ZO NG2). Eq. 6: CS = (NG2 - 1) CF. Eq. 7: f-3dB ~ sqrt(ZO GBP). Figure 5 example: RG 200, RF 850, CS 39 pF, CF 1.7 pF, NG1 = 5.25, NG2 = 24, ZO = 4.4 MHz, CF = 1.76 pF and CS = 40.6 pF calculated, 'reduced slightly ... to account for parasitics', f-3dB about 131 MHz. Used differentially on the front page (differential gain 8.5 V/V, 200 ohm total source impedance per half): 2 Vpp distortion below -95 dB up to 20 MHz, < -90 dBc through 30 MHz.
  - Figure 6 'Low-Gain Inverting Performance' (PDF p13; same data as the Low Gain Inverting Bandwidth plot, PDF p6): RF = 750, 0 ohm source, CS and CF chosen for a high-frequency noise gain of 21 using Eqs. 4-6; 'allows relatively low inverting gain applications to use the full slew rate and low input noise'. CURVE -3 dB: G = -1 116 MHz (+0.13 dB peak near 60 MHz), G = -8 155 MHz, G = -2 and G = -4 139-140 MHz (these two traces nearly coincide; their individual assignment is uncertain).
  - Noninverting gain below +12 (PDF p14, Figure 8): resistor R1 across the inputs, R1 = (RF + RS AV)/(NG - AV); retains slew rate at the cost of some noise. Noninverting Gain Flatness Tune plot (PDF p6; Figure 9), Av = +12 (21.58 dB), RF = 750: NG = 12 gives +0.1 dB at 40 MHz and rising; NG = 14: +0.1 dB at 43 MHz; NG = 16: +0.1 dB at 50 MHz; NG = 18: within +0.134/-0.1 dB to 176 MHz; NG = 20: -0.1 dB at 93 MHz (CURVE).
  - Very High Dynamic Range HF Amplifier (Figure 7, PDF p13): second stage gain -2 compensated with CS = 46 pF, CF = 1.6 pF (RG 750, RF 1.5 kohm).
- **src**: PDF pp. 1, 3, 6, 11-14, 16

## Application guidance

- Figure 1 (G = +20, RF 750, RG 39.2, 50 ohm source and termination, 50 ohm series output) is the specification circuit; effective load 89 ohm (100 ohm || 790 ohm feedback). Figure 2 (G = -40): RG 50, RF 2 kohm, +IN 95.3 ohm || 0.01 uF (caption printed 'Noninverting G = -40'). Inverting: the driving source must have very low output impedance beyond the closed-loop bandwidth (PDF p10).
- RG = 39.2 ohm suggested starting point to keep resistor noise low and parasitic effects small (PDF p19).
- Noise model (Figure 12) and Eqs. 9-11: a 45 ohm source on +IN adds Johnson noise equal to the amplifier en; with RG = RS = 50 ohm and NG > 12 ('as is typically required by stability considerations') Eq. 11 gives a total input noise of 1.4 nV/rtHz (PDF pp. 15-16).
- Very High Dynamic Range HF Amplifier (Figure 7, PDF pp. 13-14): 1:2 transformer, 200 ohm || 10 pF termination, first stage +15 (420 ohm / 30.1 ohm), input match via 6.19 kohm feedback path, second stage -2 with CS 46 pF / CF 1.6 pF (750 ohm, 1.5 kohm); 35.6 dB gain, 4.3 dB noise figure, > 55 dBm intercept to 30 MHz, about 100 MHz bandwidth; SFDR > 90 dB through 30 MHz for loads > 400 ohm.
- Front-page differential ADC driver for ADS5500 (two OPA847, 1:2 transformer, 850 ohm || 1.7 pF feedback, 39 pF, 100 ohm): 24.6 dB gain, < 5.1 dB noise figure.
- Layout: open ground/power planes under signal I/O pins; 0.1 uF within 0.25 inch of supply pins and 2.2-6.8 uF bulk; SMD resistors; feedback resistor close to the output pin or under the package; no sockets (PDF pp. 19-20).
- SPICE model available; it predicts small-signal AC and transient performance well but 'do not do as well in predicting the harmonic distortion characteristics' (PDF p15). Demo boards DEM-OPA-SO-1B (SBOU026) and DEM-OPA-SOT-1B (SBOU027) (PDF p15).
- Thermal example: OPA847IDBV, Figure 1, 85 C, 100 ohm load: PD = 10 V x 18.9 mA + 5^2/(4 (100 || 789)) = 259 mW, TJ = 124 C (PDF p19).

## Other notes, inconsistencies and uncertainties

- TIA text input capacitance '(1.2 + 2.5)pF' (PDF p11) differs from the table split (1.7 pF CM, 2.0 pF differential); the total, 3.7 pF, is the same. The impedance plot implies about 2.0 pF CM (0.1-10 MHz) and 2.2-2.4 pF differential (10-100 MHz).
- Eq. 3 on PDF p11 squares the 4kT/RF term (typo) and omits the (EN/RF)^2 term that the OPA846 version has.
- PDF p11 DC error arithmetic: '+/-0.5mV +/- 0.6uA x 12kohm = +/-7.2mV' omits the 0.5 mV (sum 7.7 mV); '+/-1.5uV/C +/- 2nA/C x 12kohm = +/-21.5uV/C' should be 25.5 uV/C (21.5 corresponds to 10 kohm). Also uses VOS drift 1.5 uV/C and IOS drift 2 nA/C (0-70 C column) and VOS 0.5 mV (25 C column).
- Figure 3 shows 12 kohm || 0.1 uF || 100 pF on +IN; the text says 0.01 uF and 100 pF.
- Table AOL typical 98 dB versus 95.4 dB on the typical open-loop plot.
- Table sourcing current 100 mA typical versus about 82 mA on the output-current-vs-temperature plot (conditions not stated).
- Table current noise 2.5 pA/rtHz typical versus 2.7 pA/rtHz label and curve on PDF p6.
- Noise plot bottom y tick printed '0' (should be 0.1). Overdrive plots label the input axis '(mV)' for volt-scale values. HD vs noninverting gain x axis last tick printed '50' for 60.
- Inverting overdrive plot (G = -40): the output follows -20 x the plotted input, so the plotted input is evidently the source EMF ahead of the 50 ohm source resistance.
- Assignment of the G = -2 and G = -4 traces on the Low Gain Inverting Bandwidth plot is uncertain (traces coincide near -3 dB).
- Related products table here: OPA842 2.6 nV/rtHz; OPA846 datasheet lists OPA842 at 2.4 nV/rtHz.
- Current-noise curve only extends to 10 MHz, so its behaviour at 10-200 MHz is not shown in this datasheet.
