## Identity and architecture
- Voltage-feedback, bipolar input ("XFCB", complementary bipolar), **unity-gain stable**, internally compensated (p1, p13). Single gain stage with unity-gain output buffer (Fig. 45, p13).
- G = +1 needs a 24.9 Ω series resistor at +IN (p14, Fig. 46). No guidance on capacitive (TIA) feedback or noise-gain shaping.
- **No TIA content** anywhere ("Optical electronics" appears only in the Applications list, p1).

## Noise
**Voltage noise:** 1.0 nV/√Hz at 100 kHz (TYP, Tables 1, 2). Curve Fig. 13 (p7; CURVE ±5%): 10 Hz 10; 100 Hz 3.8; 1 kHz 1.5; 10 kHz 1.06; 100 kHz–100 MHz 1.0 nV/√Hz. Highest plotted frequency 100 MHz; the trace is a smooth flat line from ≈50 kHz to 100 MHz with no HF rise or roll-off. 1/f corner ≈1.4 kHz (DERIVED from e² = e_w²(1 + f_c/f)).

**Current noise:** 2.6 pA/√Hz at 100 kHz with DISABLE floating; 5.2 pA/√Hz with DISABLE = +Vs (bias cancellation on) (TYP, Tables 1, 2). Curve Fig. 16 (p7; CURVE ±10%):

## Packages and layout
| Package | Body | Pitch | Exposed pad | EP connection | θJA |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 8-lead LFCSP, CP-8-13 | 3.00 × 3.00 × 0.75 mm | 0.50 mm | 1.74 × 1.45 mm nominal | "must be soldered to the ground plane" (Fig. 1 note) | ≈70 °C/W (JEDEC 4-layer, approx., p5) |
| 8-lead SOIC_N_EP, RD-8-1 | 4.90 × 3.90 mm | 1.27 mm | 2.29 × 2.29 mm | "must be soldered to the ground plane" (Fig. 2 note) | ≈70 °C/W |

- Pinout: LFCSP 1 DISABLE, 2 FEEDBACK, 3 −IN, 4 +IN, 5 −VS, 6 NIC, 7 VOUT, 8 +VS; SOIC 1 FEEDBACK, 2 −IN, 3 +IN, 4 −VS, 5 −VS, 6 VOUT, 7 +VS, 8 DISABLE (Figs. 1–2, p1). FEEDBACK is a secondary output placed beside −IN so the feedback network avoids the main output's package/bond-wire inductance and capacitive load (p13).
- Layout (p16–17): clear ground and power planes under the pins (one SOIC pad can add 0.2 pF); 0.1 µF bypass at the pins with electrolytics adjacent; a capacitor between the supplies helps PSR and distortion; keep returns of input, output termination, bypass and RG close; solder the paddle to ground.
- DISABLE pin (Table 7, p16): < 2.4 V disables; open = enabled; 4.3–5 V selects low-bias-current mode (current noise doubles to 5.2 pA/√Hz). Turn-off 100 ns, turn-on 40 ns (±5 V); off isolation −48 dB at 1 MHz.

## Absolute maximum and input protection (Table 3 p5; p13)
Supply 12.6 V; differential input ±1.2 V; differential input current ±10 mA; storage −65 to +150 °C; operating −40 to +125 °C; junction 150 °C. Internal antiparallel diode pairs clamp the differential input to ≈±1.2 V; ESD diodes to both rails handle 10 mA; use a series resistor to limit current.

## Uncertain or contradictory items
1. Supply range: Features "5 V to 12 V" vs General Description and Tables "4.5 V to 12 V".
2. Bias-current cancellation: "factor of 60" (p1), "factor of 100" (p16), "< 200 nA" (p13), "100 nA" (Features) vs Table 1 −0.1 µA typ / −1 µA max.
3. Current-noise curve measured only to 100 kHz, drawn flat to 100 MHz: HF current noise effectively unspecified.
4. HD2 at 5 MHz, 2 V p-p (G = +1, 1 kΩ): Fig. 17 ≈ −96 dBc vs Fig. 18 ≈ −92 dBc.
5. Table 1 "Input Bias Current Drift 3 nA/°C" vs Table 2 "Input Bias Offset Current Drift 2.5 nA/°C" (different parameter names).
6. No GBW spec; the open-loop curve uses RL = 100 Ω (DC gain ≈80 dB) while Table 1 uses 1 kΩ (85 dB); G·BW from Table 4 (370 MHz) exceeds the open-loop extrapolation (≈300 MHz).
7. Input capacitance is a single 4.4 pF value with no common-mode/differential definition.
