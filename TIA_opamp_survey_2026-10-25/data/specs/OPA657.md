## Noise and open loop (curves)

- Fig 13 (p10) plots e_n at 38.7 nV/rtHz (10 Hz), 6.4 (770 Hz), 5.0 (9.5 kHz) and 4.88 (40 kHz), falling to 4.77 nV/rtHz at 10 MHz, the highest frequency plotted. The 1/f corner estimated from the curve is about 600 Hz.
- i_n is drawn as a flat straight line at 1.3 fA/rtHz from 10 Hz to 10 MHz. No high-frequency rise is shown or discussed, and nothing is specified above 10 MHz.
- Fig 16 (p10) open-loop gain and phase (phase of A_OL, 0 deg at DC): 74.6 dB / -12 deg at 100 kHz; 67.3 / -65 at 1 MHz; 48.1 / -90 at 10 MHz; 38.6 / -96 at 30 MHz; 27.8 / -113 at 100 MHz; 20.9 / -135 at 200 MHz; 16.2 / -154 at 300 MHz; 8.9 / -184 at 500 MHz; 3.0 / -207 at 700 MHz; -3.6 / -231 at 1 GHz (end of plot). The 0 dB crossover is at 0.82 GHz.
- The Fig 16 gain implies about 2.2-2.5 GHz of gain-bandwidth, which is higher than the 1.6 GHz in the table. Fig 16 does not say whether it is measured or simulated.

## TIA content

- p1 example: R_F = 200 kOhm with C_F = 0.1 pF and a 12 pF diode gives a 10 MHz bandwidth (curve -3 dB about 11 MHz). It is quoted at 1.8 pA/rtHz total equivalent input noise over 10 MHz.
- 9.2 (p21-23, Fig 34): R_F = 200 kOhm, C_D = 49 pF plus 0.3 pF PCB, and C_F total 0.55 pF (0.4 pF capacitor + 0.1 pF from the resistor + about 50 fF PCB). A Butterworth design would need C_F = 0.2 pF (feedback pole at 3.5 MHz); Eq 8 gives about 4.8 MHz. Since the target is only 1 MHz, C_F was raised to 0.55 pF. The design adds input C_CM + C_DIFF = 0.7 + 4.5 pF to the diode capacitance. It was simulated at 1.7 MHz bandwidth with about 82 deg phase margin. Measured output noise (Fig 35) matches simulation (Fig 36): 60 nV/rtHz at low frequency (0.3 pA/rtHz input-referred), peaking at about 440 nV/rtHz near 5-6 MHz. A 5 uA pulse gives 239 ns rise and 224 ns fall.
- Equations: Butterworth C_F from 1/(2 pi R_F C_F) = sqrt(GBP/(4 pi R_F C_D)) (Eq 7); f-3dB = sqrt(GBP/(2 pi R_F C_D)) (Eq 8); band-limited output noise (Eq 9).
- Low-gain compensation (p15-17): shaping NG2 = 1 + C_S/C_F above the minimum stable gain. The example uses C_S = 27 pF and C_F = 2.9 pF at G = -2 and measured 170 MHz.

## Distortion and IMD

- HD2 / HD3 at 5 MHz, 2 Vpp, G = +10: -70 / -99 dBc into 200 ohm and -74 / -106 dBc into more than 500 ohm (TYP, p5).
- At 20 MHz (Fig 9): -55 / -74 dBc (CURVE).
- Two-tone IM3 at 10 MHz with 4 dBm per tone into 50 ohm is -82 dBc (p19, Fig 14).

## Uncertain or contradictory items

1. The table unit for GBW is printed as "dB" (p5).
2. The Fig 16 open-loop curve implies more GBW than the table value of 1.6 GHz.
3. Typical-characteristic plots cite "See Figure 28", which is the ESD figure; the intended reference is the test circuits in Figs 29 and 30.
4. On p5, the standard-grade offset drift prints +/-12 uV/C in the MIN column.
5. The current noise is drawn as an idealized flat line, and its origin is not stated.
