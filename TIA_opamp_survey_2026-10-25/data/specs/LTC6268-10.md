# LTC6268-10 — spec sheet extract

**Source:** `datasheets/ltc6268-10_distributor_copy.pdf` = LTC6268-10/LTC6269-10 data sheet, document code **626810f (Rev. F)**, "LT 0415" (April 2015), © Linear Technology 2015. Production data sheet. PDF page index = printed page number ("pN").

Types: TYP / MIN / MAX = 25 °C table values ("over T" = bullet rows, −40…85 °C I-grade or −40…125 °C H-grade); CURVE = pixel-digitised from a 300 dpi render; "design" = Note 8 (specified by design/characterisation, not production tested).

## Architecture
- CMOS input buffer driving a complementary bipolar input stage; rail-to-rail output (p11–12).
- **Decompensated, gain-of-10 stable** (A_V ≥ 10). For a TIA the data sheet requires $C_{IN}/C_F \ge 10$ (p13).
- Supply 3.1–5.25 V total (single or split); abs max 5.5 V. I_Q = 16.5 mA typ (15–18) per amp at 5 V; 0.39 mA shutdown.

## Noise
- $e_n$ = 4.0 nV/√Hz at 1 MHz (TYP; p3, p5); 0.1–10 Hz 12.6 µV p-p. 1/f corner stated 40 kHz (p12).
- Curves (±2.5 V, V_CM = 0.25 V; G16 + G17, p8): 9.3 nV/√Hz at 20 kHz, 5.1 at 100 kHz, 3.9 at 1 MHz, 4.0 at 10 MHz, 4.2 at 100 MHz, 4.6 at 200 MHz, 5.2 at 300 MHz, 5.8 at 500 MHz (CURVE ±0.2; plotted to 500 MHz).
- $i_n$ = 7 fA/√Hz at 100 kHz (TYP; p3, p5).
- **Current noise vs frequency, G20 (p9; ±2.5 V, V_CM = 0.25 V; linear 0–200 MHz; axis printed "ρA/√Hz" = pA/√Hz):** rises ≈ proportionally to frequency.

| f (MHz) | 10 | 20 | 50 | 100 | 150 | 200 |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| $i_n$ (pA/√Hz) | 0.89 | 1.43 | 3.49 | 7.06 | 10.2 | 12.0 |

  CURVE ±0.2 pA/√Hz. The 7 fA/√Hz spot value lies on the same line ($i_n \approx 7\,\mathrm{pA/\sqrt{Hz}}\times f/100\,\mathrm{MHz}$, i.e. +20 dB/decade); the slope eases above ≈180 MHz. **The data sheet gives no physical explanation of the rise**; p12 only says that in a TIA "$i_N$ dominates at higher frequencies" while $R_F$ noise dominates at low frequency. G20 looks measured (noisy trace, not labelled). The smooth Figs 2–3 model curves put $i_N$ ≈25 % lower (5.1 pA/√Hz at 107 MHz).

## Input
- $C_{IN}$: **differential 0.1 pF (DC to 200 MHz), common mode 0.45 pF (DC to 100 MHz)** (TYP, p3, p5). In the TIA equations $C_{IN}$ = total capacitance at the inverting node (Fig 1, p12–13).
- $I_B$ ±3 fA typ, ±20 fA max (25 °C); ±900 fA (I-grade) / ±4 pA (H-grade) over T (Notes 6, 8).
- $V_{OS}$ 0.2 mV typ, ±0.7 mV max (V_CM = 2.75 V), ±3 mV over T; text p16 says ±2.5 mV max over T (conflict). Drift 4 µV/°C.
- Input range −0.1 V to V+ − 0.5 V (IVR −0.1…4.5 V at 5 V, guaranteed by CMRR); second input stage above ≈V+ − 1.55 V. CMRR 85 dB typ; PSRR 95 dB typ.
- Differential input must stay below 2.0 V (protection conducts above; keep < 1 mA) (p16).

## Distortion
- HD2/HD3 = −91/−96 dB at 10 MHz, 2 V p-p, A_V = 10 (R_F 450 Ω, R_G 50 Ω), R_L = 1 kΩ, 5 V (TYP, p4); −67/−78 dB at 3.3 V (p6). G23 plotted only 0.1–10 MHz. No IMD/OIP3.

Design equations (p13): $\omega = \sqrt{2\pi\,GBW/(R_F(C_{IN}+C_F))}$; maximum TIA bandwidth $f_{TIA} = \sqrt{GBW/(2\pi R_F C_{IN})}$; damping $\zeta$ as printed; $C_F > \sqrt{C_{IN}/(\pi\,GBW\,R_F)}$ and $C_{IN}/C_F \ge 10$. Table 1 (min/max C_F): 20 kΩ → 60/100 fF (C_IN 1 pF), 140/500 fF (5 pF); 402 kΩ → 13/100 fF, 31/500 fF. $R_F$ noise is the smaller low-frequency term only if $R_F < 860\,\Omega$ (p12).

## Packages
- S8 SOIC-8 (θ_JA 120 °C/W; pins 1 and 4 NC for a guard ring; recommended for lowest leakage); S6 TSOT-23 (θ_JA 192 °C/W). No exposed pad on the single. Dual LTC6269-10: MS8E (θ_JA 40) and 3 × 3 DFN-10 (θ_JA 43), exposed pad = V− (p2, p18–21).
- Capacitive load > 5 pF gives appreciable ringing (p16).

## Uncertain / contradictory items
1. G21 conditions unstated; its 60 dB plateau contradicts A_OL — no usable open-loop gain/phase curve.
2. No physical explanation for the f-proportional current-noise rise; Figs 2–3 model ≈25 % below G20.
3. Fig 3 label 499 kΩ vs 402 kΩ in text/Table 1; offset limit 2.5 mV (text) vs 3 mV (table); I_SC table 90 mA vs G15 118–155 mA; TIA BW 210 MHz stated vs ≈220 MHz read; 1/f corner 40 kHz stated vs ≈70 kHz read.
