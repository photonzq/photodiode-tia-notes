## Application guidance

p15: RF ~250 Ohm best for |gain| >= 2; RF much below 100 Ohm loads the output (less swing, more distortion); buffer: short output to -IN; SMT parts, no wire-wound resistors. p14: ground plane removed near input/output pins; 10 uF tantalum + 0.01 uF per supply plus optional 0.1 uF V+ to V- for HD2; series ROUT for capacitive loads (Fig 13: ~55 Ohm at 10 pF, ~15 Ohm at 100 pF, chosen for <= 0.5 dB peaking). p13: closed-loop BW ~ 240 MHz/AV for AV > 5; fMAX = SR/(2 pi VP). p16: 'low input current noise and unity gain stability ... excellent choice for transimpedance applications'.

## Absolute maximum ratings and input protection

VS +/-6.6 V; IOUT limited by power dissipation; common-mode input V+ to V-; TJ 150 C; ESD 2000 V HBM, 200 V MM (p2). ESD diodes conduct if a large input is applied while unpowered (p16).

## Other notes, inconsistencies and uncertainties

- GBP stated as 240 MHz (p13) while the AV = +1 small-signal bandwidth is 900 MHz and AV = +2 is 260 MHz (p2).
- Output drive text says '100mA of output current' (p13) vs 90 mA typ linear output current (p1, p3).
- Thermal resistance: operating ratings 145 C/W (SOIC) and 187 C/W (SOT-23) vs application text 148 and 250 C/W (p2 vs p15).
- +/-3.3 V EC row 'ROUT Input Resistance Close Loop .05 Ohm' mixes labels; +/-5 V closed-loop ROUT is 0.3 Ohm (p3, p4).
- VN unit at +/-3.3 V printed as 'nV/ pA/rtHz' (p4).
- Package captions swapped on the connection diagram (p5).
- Not digitized: open-loop gain/phase (Fig 29/30), noise curve (Fig 20), HD vs f (Fig 25/26), |Z|OUT (Fig 31).
