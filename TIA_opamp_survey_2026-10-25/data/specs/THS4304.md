## Application guidance

p15: remove ground plane under the part; short direct feedback traces; G = +1: wide trace from output to inverting input; G = +2: RF = 249 Ohm keeps peaking ~2 dB with good layout, 499 Ohm gives ~5 dB peaking; lower RF reduces peaking but degrades distortion with heavy loads; 0.1-uF ceramic bypass close to the part. HD2: single-point ground between supply bypass caps on split supplies; single reference point for input termination and gain resistors; follow EVM layout. p16-p22: EVM schematics/BOM/layouts (split +/-2.5 V and single 5 V), differential ADC driver for ADS5500 (SFDR ~84 dB to 40 MHz, Fig 56).

## Absolute maximum ratings and input protection

Supply +6.0 V; input voltage +/-VS; differential input +/-2 V; output current 150 mA; TJ 150 C max (125 C for long-term reliability); ESD HBM 1600 V, CDM 1000 V, MM 100 V (p3). No input-protection circuit described.

## Other notes, inconsistencies and uncertainties

- Single-supply part (2.7-5.5 V): output swing 1.1-3.9 V at 5 V/100 Ohm, i.e. ~2.8 Vpp max; Fig 30 overdrive test clips at ~1.5/3.5 V (narrower than the EC swing).
- Fig 13 is titled '2ND HARMONIC DISTORTION vs OUTPUT VOLTAGE' but its y-axis label reads '3rd Harmonic Distortion - dBc' (p10).
- p6 EC (3 V) lists two slew-rate rows with identical condition 'G = +2, VO = 1-V Step' (750 and 675 V/us).
- Ordering table p2 gives DGK rails of 100; addendum p23 gives tube of 80.
- Zout EC row (0.016 Ohm at 100 kHz) carries test level A although it is a typical value.
- Other qualified version: THS4304-SP (space) listed in addendum p24.
