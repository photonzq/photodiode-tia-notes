This distributor copy is the **Chinese-language edition** of Rev. A (an old revision; later English revisions exist and may differ). Production data sheet. PDF page index = printed page number ("pN" below).

Types: TYP / MIN / MAX = table values at 25 °C unless "over T"; CURVE = read from a typical-characteristic plot (pixel-digitised from 300–600 dpi renders; uncertainty given); "text" = stated in prose.

## Architecture
- FET-input (JFET, "FastFET", XFCB process; p1, p14; p21 section titled "high-speed JFET-input in-amp"), **unity-gain-stable** voltage-feedback amplifier.
- Supply 5–10 V total, single or dual (p3–4); abs max 10.6 V (p5 Table 3).
- I_Q = 19 mA typ / 21 mA max per amp at ±5 V; 14/16 mA at +3/−2 V; power-down 1.5 mA typ (p3–4).

## Noise
- $e_n$ = 4 nV/√Hz at 100 kHz (TYP, p3–4). Fig 30 (p11, to 100 MHz): 54 nV/√Hz at 100 Hz, 11.8 at 1 kHz, 5.4 at 10 kHz, 4.24 at 100 kHz, 4.0 at 1 MHz, 3.96–4.0 from 3 to 70 MHz, ≈3.9 at 100 MHz (CURVE ±0.1). 1/f corner ≈ 8–10 kHz (derived, not stated).
- $i_n$ = 2.5 fA/√Hz at 100 kHz (TYP, p3–4). **No current-noise-vs-frequency plot and no statement about its high-frequency behaviour in this revision.** The p20 noise example treats $i_n$ as frequency-independent.

## Input
- $C_{CM}$ = 1.3 pF, $C_{DM}$ = 0.1 pF (TYP, no frequency range or method stated; p3–4). Fig 50 (p19) models $C_M$ from each input to ground and $C_D$ between the inputs; Eq. 14–15 use $C_S + C_M + C_D$.
- $I_B$ = 2 pA typ, 20 pA max (25 °C); 100 pA typ over temperature; ×1.7 per 10 °C (p3, p15). $R_{IN,CM}$ = 500 GΩ.
- $V_{OS}$ = 0.4 mV typ, 2 mV max (±5 V); drift 7 µV/°C.
- Input CM range −V_S to +V_S − 2.8 V (±5 V), −V_S to +V_S − 2.9 V (5 V) (TYP); CMRR −90 dB typ (−77 min); PSRR −72 dB typ (−67 min).

## TIA content
- Wideband photodiode preamp (p19, Fig 50–51): $V_{OUT} = I_{PHOTO} R_F/(1 + sC_F R_F)$; bandwidth at 45° phase margin $f_{45} = \sqrt{f_{CR}/(2\pi R_F (C_S + C_M + C_D))}$; required $C_F = \sqrt{(C_S + C_M + C_D)/(2\pi R_F f_{CR})}$.
- Example (p20 Table 9): R_F = 50 kΩ, C_S = 30 pF, C_F = 0.5 pF, BW ≈ 6.4 MHz; rms noise R_F 94 µV, e_n 777.5 µV, i_n 0.4 µV, total 783 µV (calculated).
- Fig 52 (p20): G = 63 V/V, R_L = 100 Ω, V_S = 10 V, 6 V p-p; −3 dB ≈ 5.8 MHz (CURVE); measured vs simulated not stated.
- Guidance: R_F 100–400 Ω for wideband voltage gain; C_F 0.5–2 pF reduces peaking (Fig 46: G = 2, R_F = 348 Ω: no C_F 2.2 dB peaking, 0.5 pF 1.3 dB, 1 pF flat); "no need to balance the inputs; minimise impedance from +IN to ground for best noise" (p20).

## Packages
- 8-lead LFCSP_VD (CP-8-2) 3 × 3 mm, 0.5 mm pitch, exposed pad ≈1.45 × 1.74 mm, θ_JA 94 °C/W, θ_JC 29 °C/W; pins 1 PD̄, 2 FB, 3 −IN, 4 +IN, 5 −V_S, 6 NC, 7 OUT, 8 +V_S (p5–6, p24).
- 8-lead SOIC_N_EP (RD-8-1) 4.9 × 3.9 mm, 1.27 mm pitch, EP 2.29 × 2.29 mm, θ_JA 79 °C/W; SOIC adds 1–1.5 dB peaking at G = +1 (p16).
- Exposed pad internally floating: connect to GND or −V_S plane, or leave floating (p6, p17). PD̄ must not float (tie to +V_S).
