For OPA855 the macromodel reproduces our design of Table A6.38 within 0.2% in bandwidth and 0.4° in phase margin, at 84 MHz and 78.8°, and its noise lies 0.3 dB below our model's, close to the agreement found at 2 kΩ (Section 5.6); at 0.10 pF of total feedback capacitance it gives 215 MHz, against 207 MHz from our model. For OPA818 it gives 115 MHz and 56.5° against our 129 MHz and 47°, and 2.2 dB more noise, in the same direction as at 2 kΩ, where OPA818's macromodel had 15.5° more phase margin and 1.6 dB more noise than our analysis.
